What is Privasea? (The FHE-Powered AI Network)

Privasea is a highly advanced DePIN and Artificial Intelligence network focused entirely on absolute data privacy. It solves one of the biggest problems in Web3 and AI: how to verify human identity and process sensitive data without exposing that data to hackers or centralized corporations.

The network utilizes Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE) mixed with Machine Learning (FHEML). In simple terms, FHE allows AI servers to perform calculations on your data while it remains completely encrypted. The server never actually "sees" your face, your passwords, or your private information, yet it can still mathematically verify that the data is authentic.
