What is Bee Maps? (Powered by Hivemapper)

Bee Maps is the fastest-growing mapping company in history. Built natively on top of the decentralized Hivemapper architecture (Solana), it transforms everyday drivers into network contributors. While Hivemapper serves as the underlying blockchain infrastructure protocol, Bee Maps operates as the commercial, user-facing application layer.

By equipping vehicles with specialized AI-enabled dashcams, Bee Maps captures high-quality, up-to-date road imagery and geospatial telemetry. This real-world physical data is processed dynamically to extract features like lane markings and road signs, building a living, breathing map that updates in days—not years like traditional corporate-owned systems (e.g., Google Maps).

The Massive Transition to USDC Rewards

In a historic move for the DePIN sector, Bee Maps officially transitioned its primary payout structure in August 2026. Moving away from compensating drivers solely in volatile $HONEY tokens, USDC (Stablecoin) is now the default reward mechanism.

💸 Understanding the USDC Payment Cycle:

1. Base Network Routing: To receive payments, your Circle Wallet (generated via the Bee App) connects to the Base (L2) network. When transferring funds to external exchanges, ensure your destination address supports USDC on Base.

2. The 7-Day Cycle: Each USDC reward period runs for exactly seven days (Starting 12:00 AM UTC Monday to 12:00 AM the following Monday). Payment processing hits your Circle Wallet starting Wednesday.

3. Regional Reward Goals: USDC payouts are dynamic based on geography. Check your app's 'Settings > Goal' to see the exact minimum kilometers required to qualify for that week's USDC pool in your specific city or region.

Data Quality & Network Rules (Swarm Consistency)

Enterprise companies like Volkswagen pay for absolute precision. Therefore, Bee Maps employs rigorous anti-fraud and data verification mechanics:

🐝 Swarm Consistency

Every physical object (like a speed limit sign) is cross-checked by the swarm. Only when multiple Bee devices confirm the exact same attributes (location, azimuth, speed) does the AI classify it as verified "ground truth."

📍 Precise Location Constraints

The mobile app absolutely requires "Precise Location (Always Allow)" and continuous Bluetooth permissions. Spoofing your GPS or using VPNs to fake a driving route will permanently ban your dashcam node.

Earning Optimizations (TCN Pro Tips)

Maximize your weekly USDC yield by understanding how the mapping algorithm prioritizes route data:

🗺️ Freshness Multipliers

Driving the exact same highway every day yields diminishing returns. Open the in-app explorer map before driving. Roads highlighted in "higher freshness tiers" (routes rarely mapped) award massive multiplier points compared to standard commutes.

📷 Perfect Dashcam Placement

The AI aggressively rejects blurry or obstructed imagery. Mount your Bee Dashcam directly in the center of your windshield, ensuring no dashboard glare or wiper blades obstruct the primary lens view.
