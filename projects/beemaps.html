📱 BeeMaps Mobile App Guide (Companion App & Wallet Binding)

The BeeMaps mobile application functions as the central management hub for your mapping nodes, tracking your fresh road coverage, GPS telemetry, and token earnings.

1
Download Official BeeMaps Companion App
Visit the official portal at BeeMaps Portal and download the official BeeMaps app available on the Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
2
Create Account & Connect Web3 Wallet
Launch the app and register using your email. Connect a compatible Web3 wallet (such as Phantom or Solana-compatible wallet) to bind your mapping rewards and payout identity.
3
Grant Precise GPS & Location Permissions
Allow "Precise Location (Always Allow)" and Bluetooth permissions. The protocol requires precise geospatial positioning to validate road telemetry and calculate mapping accuracy.
4
Track Freshness Multipliers & Daily Drops
Open the in-app explorer map before driving. Roads highlighted in higher freshness tiers award multiplied points compared to frequently mapped standard routes.
💡 Privacy Guard:

BeeMaps deploys automated on-device and edge AI blurring models. Human faces and vehicle license plates are strictly obfuscated before mapping imagery is uploaded to the decentralized network.

🚗 BeeMaps 4K Dashcam Hardware Guide (Full Node Deployment)

Deploying a dedicated 4K Dashcam turns any car, taxi, delivery vehicle, or motorbike into an automated street-level mapping node with maximum token yield.

1
Obtain the Official BeeMaps Hardware
Order the verified open-source BeeMaps Dashcam via BeeMaps Hardware Store. Each unit is equipped with a high-resolution 4K optical sensor and high-precision GNSS/GPS tracking.
2
Mount Camera with Clear Windshield Angle
Secure the dashcam to your windshield behind the rearview mirror. Ensure the lens has an unobstructed forward-facing view of the roadway and horizon without reflections from the dashboard.
3
Pair Dashcam to Phone via Wi-Fi & Bluetooth
Power the camera using your vehicle's 12V auxiliary port or USB. Open the BeeMaps app, select Pair Device, and link the hardware via Bluetooth/Wi-Fi to authorize your node.
4
Drive & Automatic Sync over Home Wi-Fi
The camera records continuously as you drive. When you return home and your phone reconnects to your home Wi-Fi, the mapping data packets sync automatically to the network, and reward tokens are credited to your connected wallet.
💡 Driver Earning Tip:

Commercial drivers, rideshare operators (Uber/Ola), and long-distance commuters achieve the highest weekly rewards due to extensive coverage of unique and rural road segments.
