What is Adreva? (Web3 Ad-Tech)

Adreva is a next-generation, Privacy-First DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network) advertising platform. It fundamentally shifts the power dynamic of the "Attention Economy" by rewarding users directly for viewing advertisements, replacing the traditional model where tech giants harvest and monetize your data without consent.

Operating entirely as a lightweight browser extension, Adreva allows users to monetize their screen space. By opting in, users verify their attention to curated ad campaigns and earn native ecosystem rewards, seamlessly blending passive income with daily internet surfing.

2

Technical Architecture (Zero-Knowledge Privacy)

Adreva utilizes cutting-edge Web3 privacy standards to ensure your browsing identity remains completely anonymous:

🛡️ Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKP)

Unlike traditional networks that extract your IP and history, Adreva uses ZKPs. It cryptographically proves to the server that an ad was viewed by a real human, without ever revealing who that human is or transmitting personal data.

💾 On-Device Data Storage

Adreva operates on a strict "No Cookies" policy. Your selected age ranges and category interests (like Crypto or DeFi) are stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB. The ad-matching engine runs directly on your device, not on a remote server.

⚙️ Granular Delivery Control

Users have absolute control over their ad experience. The dashboard allows you to define "Quiet Hours," set strict caps on "Max Ads Per Hour," and dictate your preferred viewing schedule to avoid intrusive interruptions.

🤖 Attention Tracking

The system employs sophisticated algorithms to verify that an ad is genuinely visible on your screen (Verified Views). This mitigates bot fraud and ensures advertisers only pay for real human engagement.

Reward Mechanics & Tier System

Adreva utilizes an "Attention-and-Tier-Based" reward mechanic designed to incentivize targeted engagement:

💎 Base Earning (Verified Views)

Users earn base in-app points for every verified advertisement impression they encounter while browsing normally. These points accumulate in real-time on your dashboard stats.

🚀 Tier-Based Multipliers

Your earning speed increases based on your selected interests. For example, a user in the "Silver Tier" earns at a 1.37x multiplier. Enabling more categories (like Layer 1 Blockchains, DePIN, etc.) upgrades you to the Gold Tier (1.5x) and beyond.

🏆 Gamification & Achievements

To encourage continuous engagement, the platform features a robust achievement system. Users unlock badges (e.g., 'Ad Explorer', 'Century Club') and earn bonus points for hitting specific viewing milestones.

📈 Endless Referral System

The dashboard includes a dedicated referral section. By inviting new users to install the extension, you generate passive bonus rewards based on the network effect of your referrals.
