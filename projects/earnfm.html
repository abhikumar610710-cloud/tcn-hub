What is Earn.fm? (Web2 Proxy Service)

Earn.fm is a traditional, non-crypto commercial proxy and bandwidth-sharing platform. It allows users to generate passive income by selling their unused internet bandwidth to corporate data clients.

Unlike Web3 DePIN protocols that feature native tokens or blockchain airdrops, Earn.fm operates as a standard client-server web application. The shared internet traffic is utilized by data scientists and businesses for digital services such as Search Engine Optimisation (SEO), web scraping, ad verification, market research, and application testing.

Infrastructure Risks & Legal Liabilities

Because proxy networks route third-party traffic through your home network, users must be aware of potential operational risks:

🛡️ Data Privacy

According to official documentation, the app only accesses network throughput and does not track, collect, or store personal files or browsing histories.

⚠️ ISP Account Suspension Risk

Your home IP address acts as the exit node for anonymous traffic. If your bandwidth is flagged for abusive scraping or copyright issues by third parties, your ISP may issue warnings or suspend your internet service.
