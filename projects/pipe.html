What is Pipe Network? (The Decentralized Cloudflare)

Pipe Network (developed by Permissionless Labs) is a revolutionary Decentralized Content Delivery Network (dCDN) built natively on the blazing-fast Solana blockchain.

Traditionally, massive Web2 giants like Cloudflare and Akamai monopolize how videos, websites, and apps load on your screen, relying on expensive, centralized server farms. Pipe Network completely decentralizes this infrastructure. It creates a global mesh of community-operated "hyperlocal" nodes that store and deliver cached content right from your local neighborhood. This dramatically reduces latency, cuts bandwidth costs, and makes the internet resilient against centralized outages.

Tokenomics & The Burn-to-Credit Model

The network introduces a highly deflationary economic model using its native $PIPE utility token:

🪙 1 Billion Max Supply

The token has a hard-capped maximum supply of 1,000,000,000 PIPE at TGE, with 22% strictly reserved for the community and active operators.

🔥 Burn-to-Credit Deflation

Customers (like developers or streaming apps) must buy $PIPE and permanently burn it to generate "Data Credits" for network usage, creating constant deflationary pressure.

🛠️ Proof-of-Useful-Work (PoUW)

Nodes do not mine blocks arbitrarily. Tokens are emitted and rewarded exclusively when a node provides mathematically verified bandwidth delivery.

🔒 Staking Dynamics

Node operators must stake PIPE to join the network, ensuring accountability. A portion of network fees is redistributed to stakers via the LovePIPE liquidity pool.

Active Earning: PoP Nodes vs. Guardian Nodes

Pipe Network utilizes a dual-node architecture to ensure content is delivered quickly and accurately. Users can participate in two different ways:

🛡️ Guardian Nodes (For Everyone)

This is the lightweight entry point. By installing the Pipe Guardian Chrome Extension, your browser conducts background network audits, checking the speed and integrity of the main network. Guardians accumulate points for future airdrops without needing heavy hardware.

🚀 PoP Nodes (For Pro Users)

Point-of-Presence (PoP) nodes are the heavy lifters. These are dedicated servers or high-end PCs that actually cache and deliver the video/data files to local users. They require high bandwidth and consistent uptime, earning the bulk of the Proof-of-Useful-Work rewards.
