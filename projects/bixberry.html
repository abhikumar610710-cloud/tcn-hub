What is Bixberry? (The AI Data Bridge)

Launched in August 2026, Bixberry is an innovative DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network) project designed specifically for the Artificial Intelligence sector. Traditional AI models like ChatGPT or Claude operate from centralized data centers, meaning they cannot independently fetch live, real-time web updates (such as current local news or live market prices).

Bixberry solves this by creating a massive "Residential Proxy Network". It routes AI requests through the idle internet connections of everyday people. When an AI needs fresh web data, Bixberry uses your connected node to securely fetch that public page, effectively turning your device into an "AI Data Bridge."
