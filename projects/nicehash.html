💻 Windows PC Setup Guide (NiceHash QuickMiner & Auto-Mining)

NiceHash QuickMiner automatically detects your graphics card (NVIDIA / AMD) and CPU, switching to the most profitable cryptocurrency algorithm in real time while paying you directly in Bitcoin ($BTC).

1
Create Free Account on NiceHash
Go to the official website at NiceHash Portal. Complete your account registration with email and set up Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for wallet security.
2
Download NiceHash QuickMiner Installer
In your dashboard, click Mining → Download Miner or Add ASIC. Choose NiceHash QuickMiner (recommended for beginners) or NiceHash Miner for multi-algorithm benchmarking.
3
Authorize Mining Address & Hardware Benchmark
Launch QuickMiner and log in or paste your Mining Address from your web dashboard. The software runs an automatic benchmark to identify peak efficiency settings for your hardware.
4
Start Mining & Receive Automated BTC Payouts
Click Start. Your PC will begin hashing. NiceHash calculates your accepted shares and credits Bitcoin directly into your internal wallet every 4 hours once minimum payout thresholds are reached.
⚙️ Hardware Optimization Profile:

QuickMiner includes built-in OCTune optimization presets (Lite, Medium, Efficient). These automatically tune GPU core clocks and undervolt power consumption to maintain cool operating temperatures.

📱 Android Mobile Guide: CPU Mining & Rig Monitoring

Android phones can participate in NiceHash through two distinct channels: direct mobile CPU hashing via Stratum pool connections, and 24/7 rig fleet monitoring with the official mobile manager.

1
Method A: Direct Mobile CPU Mining via Stratum
You can connect your Android smartphone's multi-core ARM processor directly to NiceHash CPU algorithms (such as RandomX) using an ARM-compatible miner APK (e.g., XMRig for Android):
Pool URL: stratum+tcp://randomxmonero.auto.nicehash.com:9200
User: YOUR_NICEHASH_MINING_ADDRESS.WorkerName
Password: x
Enter your NiceHash Bitcoin mining address as the username. The submitted mobile shares will register as an active worker directly in your dashboard.
2
Method B: Official NiceHash Mobile Rig Manager
Install the official NiceHash app from the Google Play Store on your Android phone and sign in with your verified credentials. This app serves as your mobile command center to monitor all active phone and PC workers.
3
Configure Unrestricted Battery & Thermal Guard
If running direct mobile mining, lock the app in your Recent Apps screen and set battery optimization to "No Restrictions". Keep thread usage at 50%-75% of your total CPU cores to prevent phone overheating.
4
Track Real-Time Worker Hashrate & Earnings
Both your mobile workers and desktop rigs will appear under the Rig Manager tab. Track accepted shares, speed, temperatures, and watch accumulated Bitcoin rewards update every payout cycle.
