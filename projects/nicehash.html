What is NiceHash? (Hashrate Broker)

NiceHash is fundamentally different from standard DePIN or single-coin mining pools. It operates as the world's largest global Hashrate Broker Marketplace.

It acts as an intelligent middleman. On one side are "Buyers" who bid in real-time to rent computing power for specific cryptographic algorithms (like SHA256 or Scrypt). On the other side are "Sellers" (everyday miners like you) who connect their CPUs, GPUs, or ASICs to the network.

2

Blockchain Ecosystem (Agnostic Mining)

NiceHash does not run on its own proprietary Layer-1 blockchain. Instead, it is a blockchain-agnostic infrastructure layer:

🌐 Multi-Chain Proof-of-Work

Your hardware may solve puzzles for Bitcoin, Litecoin, Dogecoin, or Ravencoin simultaneously based on buyer demand.

⚡ Stratum Connection Nodes

Miners connect directly via Stratum URLs for routing valid cryptographic shares back to buyers' selected pools.

📊 Real-Time Pay-Per-Share

Earn fractions of a coin for every valid share submitted, regardless of whether a block is found.

🛡️ Custodial Web Wallet

Rewards accumulate directly inside your secure, custodial web wallet hosted on the NiceHash platform.
