The Tech Magic: Bitcoin Lightning Payouts

Historically, paying users tiny fractions of a cent for viewing an ad was impossible due to massive banking and blockchain transaction fees. Slice solves this by utilizing the Bitcoin Lightning Network.

The Lightning Network is a Layer-2 solution built on top of Bitcoin that allows for instant, near-zero fee micro-transactions. This means Slice can afford to stream your accumulated "Sats" (Satoshis—fractions of a Bitcoin) directly to your digital wallet the moment you request a withdrawal, avoiding traditional banking bottlenecks.
