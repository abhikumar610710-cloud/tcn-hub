📱 NATIX Drive& Smartphone Setup Guide (Edge AI Camera Node)

The NATIX Drive& app turns any smartphone into an intelligent edge dashcam. Running lightweight computer vision AI directly on your phone's processor, it detects roadway events, traffic density, and obstacles to earn $NATIX tokens passively.

1
Download Official NATIX Drive& App
Visit NATIX Network Portal or search for NATIX Drive& on Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Install the official application.
2
Create Account & Bind Web3 Payout Wallet
Register with your email and connect your preferred Web3 wallet (or set up the embedded on-chain wallet) to receive accumulated points and token rewards.
3
Mount Smartphone on Windshield / Dashboard
Secure your smartphone in a stable car or bike mount with the rear camera facing the road ahead. Ensure the lens has an unobstructed forward view without dashboard reflections.
4
Start Driving Mode & Earn GEMs / NATIX Points
Tap Start Driving before you commute. The on-device edge AI analyzes road objects in real-time, accumulating GEMs for every kilometer driven, which convert into on-chain $NATIX token allocations.
🔒 100% On-Device Privacy:

NATIX's proprietary Edge AI detects metadata (such as vehicle counts, road signs, and potholes) without uploading raw video feeds. Human faces and vehicle number plates are never stored or transmitted to the network.
