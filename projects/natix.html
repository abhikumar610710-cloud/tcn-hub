What is NATIX Network? (Physical AI & DePIN)

NATIX Network is a decentralized physical infrastructure network (DePIN / DePAI) built natively on the Solana blockchain[cite: 84]. It crowdsources real-world driving footage and geospatial data to power Physical AI and autonomous driving models[cite: 84].

Instead of relying on corporate mapping cars, NATIX leverages edge computing and artificial intelligence directly on everyday drivers' devices to anonymize video streams and strip out Personally Identifiable Information (PII) before any data leaves the device[cite: 84].

Edge AI & On-Device Privacy Anonymization

Privacy is a foundational pillar of the NATIX protocol, addressing regulatory and user safety concerns head-on:

🔒 Zero PII Leakage:

Unlike traditional dashcams that record and transmit raw video streams to centralized servers, NATIX processes video feeds entirely on the user's device. The onboard computer vision AI instantly detects, blurs, and strips out Personally Identifiable Information (PII)—such as human faces and license plates—before any compressed geospatial data leaves the smartphone[cite: 84].
