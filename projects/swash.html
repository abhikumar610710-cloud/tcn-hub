What is Swash? (The Web3 Data Union)

Launched initially as a browser extension in 2019, Swash is one of the earliest and most robust Web3 Data Monetization platforms. It operates as a "Data Union," fundamentally shifting how internet data is valued. Unlike traditional ad-networks (such as Google Ads or Facebook) that collect user data without explicit permission and keep 100% of the profits, Swash allows everyday internet users to voluntarily share non-sensitive browsing data.

In return for sharing this data with advertisers and data scientists, Swash redirects a significant portion of that revenue directly back to the users in the form of $SWASH tokens. It is an opt-in ecosystem designed to reward you simply for surfing the web as you normally do, transforming your daily digital footprint into a passive income stream.

How Swash Operates (Data Pooling & Privacy)

Swash is engineered with a privacy-first architecture, ensuring that your sensitive information is never exposed:

🛡️ Sensitive Data Filtering

The Swash extension acts as a protective layer. Before any browsing behavior is pooled, the protocol automatically masks and filters out personal data, passwords, and banking information, ensuring only anonymized market trends are collected.

📊 Data Pooling Engine

Once you install the Chrome Extension, it runs silently in the background capturing basic surfing habits. These generalized signals are aggregated into a massive Data Pool, which is then purchased by enterprise data buyers.

⚖️ GDPR & Legal Compliance

Swash adheres strictly to European GDPR rules. It has completed a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) with the UK’s Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), ensuring full legal compliance and user opt-in consent.

🤖 Sybil Attack Protection

To prevent bots and fake scripts from draining the token ecosystem, Swash utilizes a robust user verification system. You must verify your profile (Email, Name, etc.) to confirm you are a real human before claiming rewards.
