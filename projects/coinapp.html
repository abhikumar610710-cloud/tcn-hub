What is the COIN App? (Geomining Ecosystem)

The COIN App is a highly interactive, gamified mobile application that allows users to earn cryptocurrency by participating in a decentralized geospatial network. Unlike background bandwidth-sharing apps, COIN requires active or semi-active physical movement.

Users literally "Geomine" the real world. By walking, driving, or commuting with the app open, your smartphone’s GPS and location data are utilized to verify geospatial location nodes. In return for validating this data, you are rewarded with an in-app currency called 'COIN', which can be converted into valuable crypto assets or high-end physical electronics.

The Foundation: XYO Network & Tokenomics

The COIN App operates as the primary data-gathering frontend for the XYO Network, a massive decentralized oracle network providing cryptographic proof of location:

The Utility Token ($XYO) $XYO is a traded ERC-20 token (on Ethereum and Polygon) used to power the XYO network. This is the primary cryptocurrency you swap your earned 'COIN' for.
Geospatial Oracles The movement data you generate while geomining helps build a trustless ledger of physical locations used for smart contracts and supply chain tracking.
HODL Rewards Users earn daily interest (HODL Rewards) simply by holding COIN in their app or maintaining a verified balance in external Web3 wallets.

SentinelX Hardware & Multiplier Tiers

To protect the network from GPS spoofers and reward dedicated users, COIN introduced physical hardware validation:

💳 SentinelX NFC Card

A physical card you tap against your phone. It verifies your location authenticity and provides a massive 12x boost to your base geomining rewards.

📡 SentinelX BLE

A Bluetooth beacon device you carry on your keychain. It provides the same 12x boost but connects wirelessly without needing to tap.

Advanced Tactics: Geoclaiming & Team Play

For users who have mastered the basics, the app offers competitive and cooperative ways to generate passive yield:

🏆 Geoclaiming & Leaderboards:

1. Geoclaim Regions: Users can invest their COIN to "claim" a specific geographical region for a week. As other users geomine in that area, the owners earn a percentage of their rewards. (Use a Geoclaim Shield to protect your own rewards from being claimed).

2. Team Lift & Socialize: Group up with other geominers to discover additional rewards, pick a unique username, and climb the global leaderboards.
