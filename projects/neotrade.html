What is NeoTrade? (Self-Custodial AI Agent Architecture)

NeoTrade is a specialized, enterprise-grade AI computational agent protocol designed for prediction markets such as Polymarket and predict.fun. Unlike conventional custodial cloud services or centralized algorithmic bot platforms, NeoTrade operates completely on client machines (macOS, Windows, and Linux) as an edge computation node. As defined in their official whitepaper: "NeoTrade is a self-custodial AI trading agent for prediction markets. It runs on your machine, trades within the limits you set, and improves with every market outcome. Your rules. Your keys. Your agent."

In legacy financial infrastructures, automated market analysis requires sending sensitive private keys, API secrets, and wallet balances to third-party web servers, exposing users to catastrophic custody drainers. NeoTrade solves this critical vulnerability by maintaining strict local key derivation and execution. All security-sensitive actions stay on your machine, private keys remain encrypted locally, and the protocol never takes custody of funds.

For everyday crypto participants, NeoTrade introduces a frictionless zero-capital pathway.
