Proxy vs. VPN Security Limitations (TCN Tips)

If you are an individual user looking for personal security, understand the difference between a Proxy and a VPN:

⚠️ Lack of Deep Encryption:

While a proxy perfectly hides your IP address, it is strictly application-based (e.g., configuring just your browser or a Python script) and usually does not encrypt your traffic payload. If your goal is system-wide protection on public Wi-Fi to secure sensitive banking passwords, a VPN is the correct tool. Proxies are designed for anonymity, control, and performance-routing rather than deep packet security[cite: 66].
