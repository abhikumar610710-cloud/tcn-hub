🌐 About Qualoo Network

Qualoo Network is a decentralized physical infrastructure (DePIN) and AI/ML analytics platform—often described as the "Google Maps" of internet quality—rewarding users for global connectivity tests, route mapping, and on-the-move telemetry.

  • Global Connectivity Mapping: Measures real-world internet quality, speed, stability, and coverage across regions.
  • Earn Rewards with Simple Tasks: Contribute diagnostics, complete tasks, and invite friends.
  • Powered by DePIN Technology: Turns everyday smartphones and probes into distributed measurement nodes.
  • Build a Transparent Network: Helps surface coverage gaps and provider performance insights.

🔗 Network

Qualoo combines distributed user probe measurements with analytical processing to evaluate connectivity conditions across networks and locations.

Global Connectivity & Route Diagnostics

Measures connectivity environment data, bandwidth performance, internet stability, and global route behavior during structured test runs.

On-the-Move & Active Node Telemetry

Tracks network changes while moving across areas or switching providers, helping surface dead zones and quality shifts.
