What is Chirp Network? (The Universal IoT Web)

Chirp Network is a highly advanced Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network (DePIN) built natively on the high-speed Sui blockchain. Its mission is to unify the highly fragmented global telecommunications landscape.

Currently, the Internet of Things (IoT) is a mess of competing protocols, restrictive cellular carriers, and incompatible smart devices. Chirp solves this by building a single, borderless, multi-protocol decentralized wireless network. By deploying physical gateway nodes (miners), community members create an encrypted radio mesh that allows smart city sensors, logistics trackers, and agricultural monitors to communicate globally, regardless of their manufacturer.
