What is Verus Network (VRSC)?

Verus is a fundamentally community-driven, open-source Layer-1 decentralized ecosystem built on a customizable cross-chain consensus mechanism called PBaaS (Public Blockchains as a Service). It operates independently, connected natively via a trustless bridge to Ethereum.

Unlike traditional Web3 protocols backed by heavy venture capital, Verus represents a true decentralized ethos. It currently focuses on core infrastructure pillars: Self-Sovereign Identity (VerusID), protocol-level MEV-resistant DeFi, zero-knowledge data encryption, and robust, scalable cross-chain architecture.

Blockchain Fundamentals & 100% Fair Launch

Verus (VRSC) operates with a maximum supply cap of 83,540,184 tokens. Its tokenomic model is a rarity in the modern crypto landscape:

100% Fair Launch There was absolutely 0% pre-mine and 0% ICO allocation. Every token in circulation is generated purely via network validation (mining and staking).
No VC Backing Verus has no traditional venture capital backing or institutional lead investors. It is sustained entirely by continuous community donations and open ecosystem grants.
Hybrid PoP Consensus Employs a "Proof-of-Power" (PoP) consensus mechanism, securely splitting block rewards 50/50 between Proof-of-Work (PoW) miners and Proof-of-Stake (PoS) stakers.

CRITICAL WALLET SECURITY (The 24-Word Seed Phrase)

Because Verus Mobile is a Non-Custodial Wallet, there is no "Forgot Password" button. Your 24-word Recovery Phrase (Seed Phrase) is the absolute Master Key to your funds. If you lose it, your funds are gone forever. Follow these strict security protocols:

⚠️ Mandatory Seed Phrase Safety Protocols:

1. Write it on Physical Paper: ALWAYS write the 24 words down on paper with a pen, in the exact 1-to-24 order. NEVER take a screenshot, photograph, or save the phrase in digital apps (WhatsApp, Google Drive, Email, Notepad). If your phone is hacked, your funds will be stolen instantly.

2. Store Duplicate Copies: Create at least two physical copies. Store one in a secure home safe, and another in a separate, trusted location to protect against fire or water damage.

3. Upgrade to Metal Backups: For long-term security, invest in a "Crypto Steel" or metal seed storage plate. Engraving your 24 words onto stainless steel protects your master key from fire, floods, and physical degradation.

4. NEVER Share Your Phrase: The Verus Foundation, customer support, or community admins will NEVER ask for your 24 words. Anyone asking for this phrase is a scammer. You only input these words directly into the official app when restoring a wallet on a new device.
