📖 What is Titan Network? (Overview & Technology)

Titan Network is a next-generation Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network (DePIN) designed to aggregate spare computing, bandwidth, and storage resources from global edge devices. By connecting personal computers, mobile smartphones, and dedicated servers into a unified network, Titan creates an open, secure, and cost-efficient Content Delivery Network (CDN) and edge storage platform.

Instead of relying on massive centralized data centers (like AWS or Google Cloud), Titan incentivizes real internet users worldwide to share their idle resources. Contributors who run Titan Edge nodes earn rewards based on their upload bandwidth, storage allocation, and overall node uptime.

⚠️ Titan Network Rules & Anti-Cheat Policies

To protect your mining score and avoid having your node penalized or banned, make sure to follow these strict community guidelines:

Crucial Dos and Don'ts:

  • No VPNs or Commercial Proxies: Running your Titan node over a VPN or datacenter proxy will trigger anti-cheat flags and result in zero rewards. Only residential and mobile IP connections are rewarded.
  • IP Limit Rule: Do not run multiple nodes of the same type on a single home IP address. Running one browser extension, one mobile phone on Wi-Fi, or one dedicated PC node per IP is the safe standard.
  • Maintain High Uptime: Intermittent disconnects degrade your node quality score. For maximum rewards, aim for 24/7 continuous operation.
  • NAT Type Matters: Devices connected with Full Cone NAT (Open NAT) receive more data transmission tasks and yield higher earnings than Symmetric NAT.
