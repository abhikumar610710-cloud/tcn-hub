The Windows client enables full node operation by allowing you to allocate idle hard drive space (TSS) and upload bandwidth (PCDN). Follow these exact steps to install the desktop client, bind your account, and optimize task performance.

Ensure your account is created at Titan Edge Platform using referral code AFD8CQ3U. Download the official Windows application installer (.exe) directly from the dashboard, run the installer, and launch the Titan Network desktop software.
2
Bind Your Registered Email Account
In the desktop client, click on the Bind tab located in the left sidebar. In the input box labeled Email, type your registered account email and hit Enter. The client will confirm your link with the status prompt: "Account linked! Earnings secure." Note: Payout wallet configuration (USDC / Titan Wallet) can also be verified and managed directly through your web console.

Navigate to the Tasks tab in the client interface to review your two operational modes:

  • TSS Task Settings: Set your custom storage path (e.g., C:\titan_network\titanL2\storage) and define your maximum allocation (e.g., 50GB – 150GB), then click Save.
