What is MinionLab? (The Next-Gen AI Data Miner)

MinionLab (formerly known as StreamAI) is a highly innovative Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network (DePIN) based out of Washington, USA. It operates at the bleeding edge of AI Data Sourcing and Monetization.

Unlike traditional bandwidth-sharing projects (which just passively scrape the internet in the background), MinionLab installs a fully autonomous AI agent—called a "Minion"—on your device. These Minions have unique digital identities and actually simulate human-like browsing behavior. They actively navigate the internet to gather real-time data, mine specialized market sentiment (like brand FUD or customer feedback), and bypass adversarial limitations to deliver high-quality datasets to AI developers who desperately need them for model training.
