📱 Android Mobile Node Setup Guide (Decentralized Subnet Provider)

The official U2U Network mobile client allows smartphone operators to tokenize idle residential internet bandwidth into real-world decentralized subnet assets, earning U2U tokens passively. Follow this complete live setup walkthrough.

1
Install U2DPN & Start Onboarding
Download and open the official U2DPN application on your Android device. Tap through the introduction screens ("Decentralized Subnet Network" and "Tokenize Bandwidth") by clicking Continue, then tap Get Started.
2
Sign In & Apply Official Referral Code
On the Sign in to get your passive income screen, input your Email address (or use direct Google login). In the Referral code (optional) field, enter Q90BH2AT to ensure your node is properly linked to the subnet incentive pool. Tap Continue with email and verify the OTP code sent to your inbox.
3
Grant Background Connection Permissions
A notification prompt reading "Stay informed while earning" will appear. Tap Allow so U2U Network can monitor your background network connection and notify you if your session drops or pauses.
4
Verify Active Provider Status & Background Settings
Once on the main screen, confirm the status indicates "Earning Active" and "Network active - Securely sharing bandwidth". In the Profile tab, verify your unique Provider ID is registered, and ensure the Auto-resume earning switch is toggled ON.
⚙️ Essential Android Background Optimization:

To keep the node sharing bandwidth 24/7 without being stopped by Android memory management:
• Go to Settings → Apps → U2DPN → Battery and select Unrestricted / No Restrictions.
• Enable Autostart permissions.
• On your Recent Apps screen, lock the U2DPN app so clearing background apps does not terminate your session.
