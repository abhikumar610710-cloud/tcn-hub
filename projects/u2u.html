What is U2U Network (U2DPN)?

U2U Network (operating as U2DPN) is a powerful DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network) project built natively on the Unicorn Ultra Network blockchain. It replaces traditional, vulnerable VPN servers by allowing everyday users to act as "Contributor Nodes" (DPN Providers) and monetize their idle internet bandwidth globally.

Privacy is the foundational pillar of the U2U ecosystem. The protocol strictly operates on an Encrypted and No Logs architecture. This guarantees that your personal traffic, browsing history, and device storage remain completely inaccessible while the background subnet routing daemon runs.
