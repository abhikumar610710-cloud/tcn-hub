What is U2U Network (U2DPN)?

U2U Network (operating as U2DPN) is an advanced Decentralized Subnet Network powered by modern Web3 technology. It allows everyday users to monetize their idle internet bandwidth by contributing to a secure, community-driven data network.

Traditional VPNs and data routing services rely on centralized servers that can be vulnerable to breaches or data selling. U2U Network reverses this model by utilizing a decentralized framework of residential nodes. When you connect your device, you act as a "DPN Provider," routing encrypted data through your unused bandwidth.

Privacy is the foundational pillar of the U2U ecosystem. The protocol strictly operates on an Encrypted and No Logs architecture. This guarantees that your personal traffic, browsing history, and device storage remain completely inaccessible and entirely private while the background daemon runs.

Network Compliance & Best Practices

To maintain a high-quality decentralized network and ensure you receive your full rewards without account penalties, please follow these standard DePIN guidelines:

⚠️ Mandatory Infrastructure Rules:

1. Maintain Natural Uptime: Utilize the "Auto-resume earning" feature to ensure your node stays connected. Frequent manual disconnections can lower your node reliability score.

2. No VPNs or Proxies: The network demands genuine residential IP addresses. Running the U2U app behind a commercial VPN or Datacenter IP will block your data sharing capabilities and halt your earnings.

3. One Node per Network: For optimal earning rates, do not run multiple devices on the exact same Wi-Fi connection, as they will compete for the same bandwidth pool. Separate them (e.g., PC on Wi-Fi, Phone on Mobile Data).
