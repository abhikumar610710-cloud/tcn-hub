What is 3DOS? (Decentralized Manufacturing)

3DOS is a groundbreaking Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network (DePIN) built on the Sui blockchain. Unlike most web3 projects that focus strictly on digital data or wireless routers, 3DOS is revolutionizing the actual, physical manufacturing industry through peer-to-peer 3D printing[cite: 44].

Currently, global manufacturing relies on massive centralized factories, international shipping, and massive carbon emissions. 3DOS eliminates this entirely. It acts as a routing layer (an "operating system" for manufacturing) that takes a digital 3D design and routes it to the idle 3D printer physically closest to the buyer. This completely cuts out international shipping costs, avoids physical waste, and eliminates minimum order quantities (MOQs)[cite: 44].

Creator Copyrights & NFT Royalty Distribution

One of the biggest problems in 3D printing is intellectual property theft. 3DOS solves this natively on the blockchain:

🎨 Tokenized Designs

When creators upload their digital creations to the 3DOS network, the CAD files and designs are securely minted as NFTs[cite: 44].

💰 Automated Royalties

This integration ensures automated, tamper-proof royalty payments directly to the creator every single time that specific item is printed anywhere in the world[cite: 44].

🛡️ Bypassing Legal Friction

By automating payouts via smart contracts on Sui, creators bypass traditional billing overhead, licensing lawyers, and international banking fees[cite: 44].

🪙 The $3DOS Token

The native utility token handles all these micro-transactions. Buyers pay for manufacturing with tokens, and printer operators earn them for fulfilling the job[cite: 44].
