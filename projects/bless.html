What is Bless Network? (The World's Shared Computer)

Bless Network is a decentralized edge-computing DePIN platform built on the Solana blockchain[cite: 94]. It positions itself as the world's first "Shared Computer," utilizing the idle processing power and internet bandwidth of everyday consumer devices (laptops, computers, and mobile phones) to execute AI and data processing tasks[cite: 94].

Instead of relying entirely on expensive, centralized data centers, Bless Network aggregates millions of idle CPU/GPU resources globally. This grants developers access to supercomputer-grade capabilities for AI model training, data processing, and low-latency workloads at a fraction of traditional costs[cite: 94].

The Dual-Token System ($TIME & $BLESS)

The protocol operates on a unique dual-token structure designed to separate network contribution from governance and liquidity:

⏱️ $TIME Token (Contribution Proof)

Generated passively when you run browser nodes or share compute resources. It acts as an on-chain proof of your exact contribution to the network[cite: 94].

🪙 $BLESS Token (Native Asset)

The primary native and governance token of the ecosystem. Earned $TIME tokens can be converted into $BLESS, which is actively traded on cryptocurrency markets (such as Binance Alpha platforms)[cite: 94].
