What is Roam? (The "Airbnb of WiFi")

Roam (formerly known as MetaBlox) is a revolutionary Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network (DePIN) natively built on the Solana blockchain. It fundamentally acts as the "Airbnb of WiFi," allowing users to monetize their excess internet bandwidth while providing secure internet access to global travelers.

Unlike traditional telecommunications monopolies that control centralized cell towers, Roam crowdsources its infrastructure. It utilizes advanced OpenRoaming technology, which connects millions of fragmented private WiFi networks worldwide into a single, seamless global web. Once authenticated on the Roam app, users connect to these global hotspots automatically—without constantly re-entering passwords or risking their data on unsecured public connections.
