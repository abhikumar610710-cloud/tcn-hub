What is PacketStream? (Core Infrastructure)

PacketStream is a peer-to-peer (P2P) residential proxy network launched originally in 2018. It operates as a data-sharing marketplace connecting two distinct user bases: Packeters (individuals who monetize their unused internet bandwidth) and Customers (businesses needing clean residential IP addresses for web scraping and market research).

The software runs quietly in the background of your computer, routing enterprise web requests through your home network connection while compensating you per gigabyte transferred.
