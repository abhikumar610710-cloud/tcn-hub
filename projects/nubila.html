What is Nubila Network? (The Physical Data Infrastructure for AI)

Nubila is an advanced, enterprise-grade Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network (DePIN) and Weather Oracle designed to transform real-world environmental signals into trusted, machine-readable intelligence. In modern artificial intelligence and climate modeling, accurate hyperlocal weather data, carbon analytics, and physical sensor metrics are critical yet severely fragmented. Nubila solves this physical data bottleneck by building a community-driven sensor and oracle network. By deploying IoT weather stations and connecting everyday mobile devices, the protocol aggregates trusted environmental inputs, feeding decentralized AI models, agricultural planning engines, and smart energy markets with verifiable real-world metrics.

For individual node operators and community contributors, Nubila provides a completely free, barrier-free passive income mechanism.
