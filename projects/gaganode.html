🌐 What is GaGaNode? (DePIN Bandwidth Market)

GaGaNode is a decentralized residential IP and bandwidth marketplace developed by Meson Network, operating natively within the DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network) sector.

The platform combats the global shortage of IPv4 addresses by allowing everyday users to monetize their idle internet connections and spare electronics. When you install the client or SDK, your device becomes an "Edge Node," securely renting out a fraction of your bandwidth to AI companies, big data researchers, and enterprise scrapers who require clean residential IPs for data assessment.

🛡️ Network Rules, IP Overlap Warning & Security

Strict One-Device-Per-IP Limit: Only ONE device per public IP address (or Wi-Fi network) is permitted. Connecting both a laptop and a mobile phone to the same home Wi-Fi triggers an "IP Overlap" error and risks account suspension.

Safe Multi-Device Strategy & VPN Restriction: To run multiple devices legally, connect one device to your home residential Wi-Fi and operate the second on an independent cellular SIM data network. Never use commercial VPNs or datacenter proxies to mask your node IP, as automated anti-cheat systems will ban the account.

❓ FAQ

What is GaGaNode and how does it relate to Meson Network?

GaGaNode is a decentralized residential IP marketplace developed by Meson Network. It allows users to monetize idle internet bandwidth and earn rewards tied to the $MSN token ecosystem.

How do I earn and claim $MSN tokens?

Running nodes accumulates internal GaGa Credits every 10 to 30 minutes, which are periodically mapped and exchangeable into the primary $MSN ERC-20 token on supported cross-chain networks.

Can I run multiple GaGaNode devices on the same Wi-Fi?

No. GaGaNode strictly enforces a one-device-per-IP rule. Running multiple devices on the same network causes an "IP Overlap" error and risks account suspension. Use separate cellular SIM data for additional devices.

What devices are compatible with GaGaNode?

GaGaNode supports Windows, Linux, Mac, Android smartphones, TV boxes, and Raspberry Pi or ARM Single Board Computers via GUI client or terminal SDK installation.

Where can I access the official GaGaNode portal?

You can access the official dashboard, Node Token, and setup guide at gaganode.com.
