What is GaGaNode? (DePIN Bandwidth Market)

GaGaNode is a decentralized residential IP and bandwidth marketplace developed by Meson Network. It operates natively within the DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network) sector.

The platform combats the global shortage of IPv4 addresses by allowing everyday users to monetize their idle internet connections and old electronics. When you install the software, your device becomes an "Edge Node," securely renting out a fraction of your bandwidth to AI companies, big data researchers, and enterprise scrapers who require clean residential IPs for data assessment.

Network Rules & The "IP Overlap" Warning

To maintain a high-quality residential proxy pool, GaGaNode enforces strict deployment rules:

⚠️ Mandatory Security Guidelines:

1. Strict IP Limits: Only ONE device per public IP address (or Wi-Fi network) is permitted. If you connect both a laptop and a mobile phone to the same home Wi-Fi and start mining, it will trigger an "IP Overlap" error and potentially ban your account.

2. Multi-Device Strategy: To run multiple devices legally, connect one to your home Wi-Fi and operate the second on an independent cellular data network (SIM).
