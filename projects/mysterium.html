What is Mysterium Network (MystNodes)?

Mysterium Network is a pioneer in the Web3 infrastructure space, functioning as a massive decentralized Virtual Private Network (dVPN) and Peer-to-Peer (P2P) protocol. Currently supporting a massive global network of over 45,000 active nodes routing 2.82+ Petabytes of data across 104 countries, it operates as a borderless open-source marketplace.

Everyday users (Node Hosts) install the MystNodes software to rent out their idle internet bandwidth. Conversely, privacy-seeking consumers and vetted B2B partners purchase this bandwidth to access a secure, censorship-resistant internet. Node runners are compensated directly in the network's native crypto token for their uptime and data transfer.

Blockchain Mechanics & Layer-2 Settlement

Mysterium utilizes a sophisticated dual-layer blockchain architecture to manage identities and process millions of micro-transactions efficiently:

🌐 Ethereum Foundation (Layer 1)

The network is originally rooted on the Ethereum mainnet, where its foundational smart contracts, node identities, and security protocols are permanently registered.

⚡ Polygon Network Integration

To eliminate high Ethereum gas fees, everyday transactions and reward distributions are processed on the Polygon (MATIC) network, ensuring fast and cheap token transfers.

💸 Hermes Micropayments (L2)

Your earnings do not go straight to your wallet; they collect in the off-chain "Hermes Layer-2" settlement contract. Once you hit the threshold (5 $MYST), the contract auto-transfers the funds to your Polygon wallet without manual gas fees.

🔑 Registration Fee (On-Chain)

To register a new node identity on the blockchain and prevent spam, the network requires a tiny one-time on-chain fee (usually $0.30 - $1.00 paid in $MYST or MATIC) during the initial setup process.

Network Compliance & IP Regulations

To maintain network quality and ensure fair compensation, Mysterium enforces specific connection rules:

📡 Connection Rules & Fair Usage:

1. Residential IPs Preferred: While running a node on a VPS/Data Center is technically allowed (and documented for Linux servers), Residential Wi-Fi connections receive significantly higher demand and B2B traffic, leading to much higher earnings.

2. One Node Per Public IP: For security and routing optimization, the network strictly limits operation to ONE MystNodes instance per external public IP address. Running multiple nodes on the same home router will cause network collisions.

Hidden Earning Optimizations (TCN Pro Tips)

Maximize your $MYST token yield by configuring your NodeUI dashboard and local network properly:

⚙️ Automatic Pricing Selection

Inside the NodeUI, always select "Automatic Pricing." The network algorithm will dynamically adjust your price-per-GB based on your country's supply and demand, ensuring you receive maximum traffic routing.

🔓 Open NAT Type (Crucial)

Nodes with a "Symmetric" or "Strict" NAT type receive very little traffic. You must access your home router settings and enable UPnP (Universal Plug and Play) or configure port forwarding to achieve an "Open" NAT type.

⏱️ 24/7 Uptime Reputation

The routing algorithm heavily favors reliability. Nodes that remain online 24/7 are classified as "Prime Nodes" and are always the first to be allocated new VPN consumers and B2B data requests.

🦊 Wallet Connection

Ensure you connect a Polygon-compatible EVM wallet (like MetaMask) to your dashboard. When your Hermes L2 balance crosses 5 $MYST, the auto-withdrawal feature will securely settle your earnings on-chain.
