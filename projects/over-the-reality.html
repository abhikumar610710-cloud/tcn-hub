🌍 Project Overview & Spatial DePIN Vision

Over The Reality (OVER) is building a decentralized spatial computing infrastructure that bridges physical real-world locations with persistent Augmented Reality (AR) experiences. Rather than relying on centralized mapping corporations, OVER crowdsources high-precision 3D spatial data directly from everyday smartphone users and 360-degree camera operators.

Through its Map2Earn protocol, contributors scan physical landmarks, streets, and indoor spaces to construct a global 3D digital twin. This infrastructure powers centimeter-level Visual Positioning System (VPS) localization for robotics, Physical AI, gaming, and location-based AR commerce.

⚙️ Core Ecosystem Architecture

🗺️ Map2Earn Protocol

Scan real-world locations using a smartphone rear camera or compatible 360° cameras (such as Insta360) to build OVRMaps and earn $OVR rewards with active area boosters.

⬡ OVRLands (Hexagonal NFTs)

Earth's surface is divided into a global grid of hexagonal virtual land parcels (identified by 3-word coordinates like likewise.cake.joey) that users can own, trade, and host AR content on.

🎯 Visual Positioning System (VPS)

Delivers centimeter-accurate indoor and outdoor spatial localization—far more precise than standard GPS—for real-time AR experiences, robotics, and Physical AI.

🛠️ AR Creation & Unity SDK

Includes an in-app AR builder, no-code web builder, and Unity SDK enabling creators and brands to deploy geolocated 3D experiences without heavy coding.

🚀 Step-by-Step Guide: How to Start Mapping

1

Access the Open Portal & Download OVER App

Visit the official Over The Reality portal (no invite code required) and install the OVER mobile application on Android or iOS, or sign in via marketplace.ovr.ai.

2

Configure Profile & Web3 Wallet

Create your account to receive your unique UUID, link your social accounts, and connect an EVM-compatible wallet (MetaMask / WalletConnect) to manage OVR across Ethereum, BSC, and Polygon.

3

Select Map2Earn Mode (Phone or 360 Cam)

Open the Map2Earn tab inside the app, check local area reward rates and your multiplier (e.g., x1.00 Booster), and tap Start mapping while walking smoothly around an unmapped spot.

4

Track Rewards in Mapper Dashboard

Monitor your created OVRMaps and lifetime $OVR breakdown across Standard mapping rewards, 360 mapping rewards, and Extra rewards directly inside your dashboard.
