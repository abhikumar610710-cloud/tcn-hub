⛏️ About Kryptex

Operating since the mid-to-late 2010s, Kryptex is an established computational power brokerage and auto-mining platform that rents out CPU, GPU, and ASIC hardware capacity for blockchain compute workloads.

Instead of manually configuring pools for every coin, users connect supported hardware and earn rewards tracked in Bitcoin or supported payout options.

Kryptex is blockchain-agnostic, allowing miners to supply compute power across supported algorithms while managing balances and rigs centrally.

🌐 Network

Kryptex routes compute and pool workloads across desktop rigs, mobile devices, and ASIC setups with flexible connection allowances.

Unrestricted Device Scaling

Connect multiple PCs, phones, or ASIC rigs under an account without rigid per-IP device caps.

VPN & VPS Routing

Supports routing through VPN or VPS setups when needed for connectivity and network stability.

💻 Devices

Kryptex offers dedicated software across Windows desktops, compatible Android devices, GPU mining tools such as KRig, and ASIC management utilities.

Hardware Baselines: Windows mining generally requires Windows 10/11, sufficient system RAM (such as 4–8GB+), and a compatible GPU (such as 4GB+ VRAM), while Android mining requires compatible hardware and OS versions.

🛡️ Safety

Thermal & Power Safety: Use GPU undervolting/power limits to keep temperatures controlled, ensure adequate airflow and power supply headroom, and avoid unsafe battery conditions on continuous mobile setups.

Fake Token & Phishing Warning: Kryptex does not have an official native token—avoid unofficial tokens claiming to be Kryptex on decentralized exchanges, and download software only from official sources.

Account Security: Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) and verify payout addresses before confirming withdrawals.

⚡ Earning

Earnings depend on hardware efficiency, current mining profitability, uptime, and electricity cost per kWh.

BTC & Regional Fiat Options

Accumulate rewards and withdraw in Bitcoin (BTC) or supported regional fiat payout methods depending on availability.
