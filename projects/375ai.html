What is 375.ai? (Physical AI & Sensory Network)

375.ai is a Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network (DePIN) built natively on the Solana blockchain. Founded in 2022, it functions as the ultimate observed-world data layer for physical AI systems.

The project bridges the massive gap between digital AI knowledge and real-world dynamics. By building a decentralized "sensory nervous system," 375.ai utilizes crowd-sourced edge computing devices to generate real-time, anonymized metadata from physical environments. This high-fidelity data—covering traffic vectors, pedestrian flow, and infrastructure usage—acts as the primary training "ground truth" for autonomous vehicles, robotics, and spatial computing developers.

Tokenomics & The $EAT Utility Token

The network operates using its native utility token on Solana, establishing a highly deflationary economic model:

🪙 The $EAT Token

$EAT (Edge Activity Token) has a fixed maximum supply of 1,000,000,000 (1 Billion) tokens.

🔥 Buy-and-Burn Mechanism

Enterprise buyers (logistics firms, municipal governments) purchase physical data using data credits. This permanently removes $EAT tokens from circulation, decreasing supply over time.

🪂 Airdrop & Community Allocation

40% of the total supply is designated for community incentives. The Mainnet TGE concluded on October 29, 2025.

🔒 Staking & Validation

Network security, node validation, and data verification require token staking. Long-term lockups (6-12 months) provide yield bonuses ranging from 50% to 100%.

Active Earning via Hardware & Mobile Nodes

The network expands and collects data through two primary, highly distinct consumer-facing products:

📹 375edge (Enterprise Hardware)

An industrial edge-compute node equipped with an NVIDIA Jetson processor and up to 6 HD cameras. Deployed in high-value public spaces to perform heavy edge AI inference.

📱 375go (Free Mobile App)

A free mobile application allowing users to passively map geographical hexagonal cells via GPS/cellular data to earn $EAT tokens as they move.

🧠 On-Device AI Privacy

Instead of streaming raw video to the cloud, on-device AI extracts only structured metadata (e.g., vehicle counts) while strictly filtering out personal identity to protect privacy.

📈 Earnings Distribution

Tokens are distributed based on localized uptime, the volume of data classified via on-device inference, and referral multipliers.
