The USD.infra Vault Mechanics (Yield Generation)

DAWN's core economic engine is the USD.infra Vault. This is how the protocol generates sustainable, non-inflationary yield for participants:

💵 1. Capital Deposit

Users deposit capital into the USD.infra Vault. In return, they are issued 'sUSD.infra' tokens representing their fractional share of the vault's assets.

🏗️ 2. Infrastructure Financing

The Vault routes this stablecoin capital directly to vetted AI infrastructure operators to build out new servers, GPUs, and data centers.

📜 3. Contract Payments

As these operators generate revenue from AI companies using their hardware, they route contracted cashflows back into the Vault.

📈 4. Yield Distribution

These returning contract payments automatically increase the underlying value of the 'sUSD.infra' tokens, generating passive yield for depositors.
