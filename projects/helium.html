📱 Helium Mobile App Guide (Discovery Mapping & $MOBILE Rewards)

Helium Mobile combines carrier network offloading with user-driven coverage discovery. By keeping Discovery Mapping active on your smartphone as you travel, you validate wireless coverage and earn daily $MOBILE token rewards.

1
Install Official Helium Mobile / Helium Wallet App
Visit Helium Mobile Portal or search for Helium Mobile on the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. For on-chain asset management, also download the official Helium Wallet app.
2
Set Up Solana-Compatible Identity
Create or import your wallet. Helium runs on the high-performance Solana blockchain. Back up your secret 12 or 24-word seed phrase securely.
3
Enable Discovery Mapping & Location Permissions
Inside the Helium Mobile app, navigate to Mapping and toggle ON Discovery Mapping. Set location access to "Allow All The Time" so the app can register coverage hexes as you commute.
4
Qualify for Daily Epoch Mapping Drops
To qualify for daily reward shares, your phone must record a minimum threshold of qualifying location events across distinct hexagonal mapping zones throughout the day. Accumulated $MOBILE rewards can be claimed directly into your wallet.
💡 Privacy Guard:

Discovery Mapping uses randomized location hex verification. The protocol does not link your personal identity to travel history, only verifying that physical coverage exists in mapped zones.

📡 LoRaWAN IoT Hotspot Guide (Proof-of-Coverage & $IOT Rewards)

Deploying a physical LoRaWAN hotspot provides long-range wireless connectivity (LongFi) for billions of IoT devices, sensors, and asset trackers, earning automated Proof-of-Coverage rewards.

1
Obtain a Certified LoRaWAN Hotspot
Select an approved third-party Helium manufacturer (such as SenseCAP, Bobcat, RAK Wireless, or Linxdot) matching your country's radio frequency (e.g., US915, EU868, IN865).
2
Optimize Antenna Placement & Height
Mount the fiberglass antenna as high as possible (rooftop or high-floor window) with a clear line-of-sight view of your surroundings. Higher elevation maximizes beacon broadcasts and witness connections.
3
Connect to Power & Ethernet
Power the hotspot and connect it via Ethernet cable (recommended for minimal packet latency) or stable Wi-Fi. Open the manufacturer’s pairing app to link the device to your Helium Wallet address.
4
Assert Location & Begin Mining $IOT
Assert your hotspot location on-chain. The device will automatically participate in daily Proof-of-Coverage (PoC) challenges, transmit IoT sensor data packets, and deposit $IOT token rewards into your Solana wallet.
💡 Ultra-Low Power Consumption:

Helium IoT hotspots consume only ~5W of power (similar to a standard LED bulb), making them among the most energy-efficient passive DePIN hardware nodes in existence.

🔒 Network Rules & Data Privacy Guarantee

Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Standard: Helium replaces traditional telecommunications monopolies with community-owned wireless nodes. The network protocol strictly handles RF transmission packets and location telemetry without accessing private user data.

⚠️ Network Compliance & Fair-Use Guidelines:

  • No Location Mocking / Spoofing: Helium uses algorithmic radio signal analysis and GPS cross-validation. Using mock GPS apps or asserting false hotspot locations will place your device on the denylist, forfeiting rewards.
  • Antenna Matching: Ensure your antenna gain (dBi) and cable loss are correctly entered in your hotspot configuration to comply with regional radio regulatory standards.
