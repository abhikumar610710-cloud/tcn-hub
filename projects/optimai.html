📱 Android Mobile Setup Guide (OptimAI Edge Node)

Turn your Android smartphone into an AI edge powerhouse. The OptimAI Edge Node client shares idle mobile/Wi-Fi connection resources safely in the background.

1
Download Official Android App
Register on OptimAI Portal with code 652D3F7D. In your dashboard, open Download Nodes → OptimAI Edge Node and tap Google Play to install the official app.
2
Log In & Authorize Edge Sharing
Launch the app, log in using your registered credentials, and tap Start Edge Node to connect to the decentralized AI data layer.
3
Configure Unrestricted Battery Settings
To maintain continuous node execution without OEM battery killer interference:
  • Go to phone Settings → Apps → OptimAI → Battery.
  • Select Unrestricted / No Restrictions (Don't optimize).
  • Enable Autostart permissions.
  • Lock OptimAI in your Recent Apps screen with the padlock icon.
4
Verify Active Edge Telemetry
Confirm the app status reads "Active / Sharing". Your mobile uptime and edge rewards automatically sync with your unified dashboard balance.
