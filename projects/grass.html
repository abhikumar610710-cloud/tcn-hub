What is Grass Network? (Decentralized AI Data Layer)

Grass Network is the underlying infrastructure that powers AI models by creating a decentralized web scraping protocol. In today’s digital economy, artificial intelligence requires massive amounts of data to learn and generate outputs. Traditional AI companies pay centralized data centers billions to scrape public websites, but this approach is increasingly blocked by anti-bot protections and regional restrictions.

Grass solves this by leveraging everyday residential internet connections. By running a lightweight node, you allow the network to route micro-requests through your IP address to view and index public websites exactly as a normal user would. This data is then cleaned, organized, and sold to AI labs to train the next generation of artificial intelligence.

The protocol is strictly designed for privacy and security. Grass cannot see your personal data, cannot monitor your browsing history, and does not have access to your hard drive. It strictly utilizes a fraction of your unused bandwidth (less than 0.3%) to perform public web indexing in a completely sandboxed environment.

How Grass Operates (Epochs & Automated Scraping)

Running a Grass node requires zero technical maintenance. Once installed, it operates completely autonomously:

📅 Epoch-Based Cycles

Grass measures node contributions in dedicated periods known as "Epochs" (e.g., Epoch 4, Epoch 5). Rewards, snapshots, and network earnings are finalized and distributed at the conclusion of each active Epoch.

⚡ Zero-Intervention Scraping

Once the extension or app is connected to your network, the AI data routing operates silently in the background. You do not need to click ads, complete tasks, or leave your screen on to earn points.

📊 Real-Time Dashboard Analytics

Operators can track their exact contributions via the live dashboard, which provides daily granular statistics on total Network Earnings, Uptime Earnings, and connected device health.

🔒 Bandwidth Protection

The routing engine automatically throttles its usage if you are playing competitive games, streaming 4K video, or downloading heavy files, ensuring your personal internet experience is never disrupted.

Network Compliance & Anti-Cheat Verification

AI data buyers demand high-quality, organic residential traffic. To ensure you receive full points and avoid shadow-bans, operators must adhere to strict infrastructure rules:

⚠️ Mandatory Infrastructure Rules:

1. Network Quality Scoring: Your earnings are directly tied to your Network Quality Score (visible as 0%, 50%, 75%, 85%, or 100%). Nodes maintaining an 85% or higher score receive priority data routing and maximum point generation.

2. Strict Ban on Proxies/VPNs: Using commercial VPNs, Tor exit nodes, or Datacenter IPs (VPS) will result in a 0% network score and account suspension. The network only values genuine home IP addresses.

3. One Account per Network: Connecting multiple Grass accounts to the same home Wi-Fi will divide the bandwidth and penalize the earnings of all connected accounts. Run multiple devices on different networks (e.g., Home Wi-Fi + Cellular Data) for optimal results.
