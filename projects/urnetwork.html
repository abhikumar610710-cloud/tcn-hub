What is URnetwork? (The Decentralized VPN Overlay)

URnetwork (UR.io) is a cutting-edge Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network (DePIN) that functions as a highly secure, peer-to-peer privacy routing protocol. In simple terms, it is a next-generation Decentralized VPN (dVPN).

Traditional consumer VPNs (like NordVPN or ExpressVPN) route your internet traffic through centralized corporate servers. This creates a massive single point of failure and privacy risk, as the VPN company holds all your data logs. URnetwork completely disrupts this model. Instead of corporate server farms, URnetwork routes encrypted web traffic dynamically through the residential internet connections of everyday users globally, creating an untraceable, "whole-internet" encryption mesh.
