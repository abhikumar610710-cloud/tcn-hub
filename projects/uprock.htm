What is UpRock? (AI Data Infrastructure)

UpRock is a robust Decentralized AI Data Infrastructure Network (DePIN) operating exclusively on the Solana (SOL) blockchain. It functions as a powerful "knowledge abstraction layer," connecting everyday users with enterprise AI companies.

By utilizing the idle internet bandwidth of your mobile phones and computers, UpRock facilitates large-scale web-scraping, real-time data mining, and AI model development. In exchange for providing this infrastructure, users are directly rewarded with the network's native cryptocurrency, creating a seamless bridge between Web3 participation and AI advancement.
