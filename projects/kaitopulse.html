🛡️ What is Kaito Pulse? (The "Fake Guru" Killer)

Kaito Pulse is an open-source Chrome browser extension built by Kaito AI in the emerging sector of InfoFi (Information Finance) and Data Intelligence.

Instead of relying on doctored profit screenshots on X (formerly Twitter), Kaito Pulse acts as a native social intelligence overlay—displaying mathematically verified, real-time trading positions (from platforms like Polymarket and Hyperliquid) and wallet balance thresholds directly beside a user's post on the X timeline.

🔐 zkTLS Architecture & Zero-Knowledge Verification

Kaito Pulse verifies trading track records and liquidity without requiring API keys, passwords, or private keys:

🔑 zkTLS Browser Proofs (Powered by Primus)

🔒 Dynamic Privacy Controls & Wallet Hygiene

Granular Privacy Toggles: You remain in full control of what is shown publicly. You can connect, verify, or unlink wallets and accounts anytime.
