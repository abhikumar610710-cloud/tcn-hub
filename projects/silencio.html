What is Silencio AI? (Voice Data & Noise Mapping)

Silencio AI is a prominent decentralized physical infrastructure network (DePIN) that rewards users for collecting environmental audio and voice data. Originally launched to map global noise pollution levels using crowd-sourced smartphone data, it has scaled into a multi-sensor network.

Today, user-contributed human voice samples are utilized by frontier AI labs to train localized accents, rare languages, automated speech recognition (ASR), and robotics systems. It bridges the gap for regional linguistic data that traditional voice AI models cannot hear.

Strict Payout Rules & GDPR Compliance

To maintain high data integrity for enterprise AI labs, Silencio enforces explicit operational requirements:

⚠️ Mandatory Payout Requirements:

1. Minimum Recording Threshold: Users must complete a minimum of 10 minutes of verified recorded voice data before becoming eligible for payouts.

2. Whitelist Approval: Account profiles require whitelist approval before reward distribution, and campaign payments follow a strict FIFO (first in, first out) logic as enterprise clients purchase data.

3. GDPR Compliance: Data registries sync with an on-chain immutable consent tracking ledger to legally protect user privacy under strict GDPR and CCPA standards.
