What is KGeN? (Kratos Gamer Network / IndiGG Ecosystem)

KGeN (Kratos Gamer Network), evolved from the pioneering IndiGG gaming collective, is a premier decentralized Proof-of-Gamer (PoG) network and multi-chain gamer identity infrastructure. Supported by top global institutions including Polygon, Animoca Brands, and Peak XV, KGeN shifts value back to players by authenticating in-game data, tournament credentials, and daily active participation.

Instead of treating casual and competitive players as ad-consuming endpoints, KGeN enables participants to tokenize and monetize their digital gameplay footprint. Through its native Android mobile app, players verify their genuine human identity, complete curated gaming quests, and earn verifiable on-chain assets and exchange-tradable $KGEN tokens without upfront hardware expenses.
