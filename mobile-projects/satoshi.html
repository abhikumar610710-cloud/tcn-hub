Origin & Protocol Genesis: What is Satoshi App?

Satoshi App (originally launched during the test phase as Satoshi BTCs) represents one of the most successful community-driven token distribution networks in decentralized finance history. Engineered as an intellectual homage to Bitcoin creator Satoshi Nakamoto, the protocol was architected to eliminate the severe financial exclusion and whale centralisation seen in traditional Venture Capital (VC) token allocations.

Rather than holding private presales or early institutional rounds, the developers utilized Satoshi App as a sovereign, zero-investment faucet to distribute CORE, the foundational gas and governance asset of the Core DAO Layer-1 blockchain. Across its historical mining period, millions of global participants accumulated verified allocations directly from smartphones without paying gas fees or burning physical device resources.

Following the successful mainnet deployment and tier-1 exchange listings of CORE in early 2023, the platform underwent an architectural upgrade: transitioning from a single-token mining utility into a permanent Decentralized Multi-Airdrop Hub. Today, vetted Web3 protocols leverage Satoshi App's massive, KYC-verified human network to distribute upcoming governance tokens directly to genuine community contributors.

The Multi-Airdrop Engine: How Block Rounds Work

Satoshi App does not rely on hardware cryptographic hashing (Proof of Work) inside mobile clients. Instead, the network operates an automated, server-side block-round allocation engine synchronized with active Web3 protocol timelines:

⚙️ 1. Hourly Block Rounds & Consensus Cycles

Participating airdrop campaigns (such as OpenEX / OEX and decentralized ecosystem pilots) dispatch reward tokens every hour through sequential block rounds. Operators receive proportional point allocations computed against their personal Hash Power score.

⏱️ 2. The 25-Round Auto-Suspension Mechanism

To ensure active human participation and eliminate dormant or abandoned node exploitation, the protocol enforces a hard 25-round queue ceiling. If an operator fails to tap Claim before 25 block rounds conclude (~24 hours), automated reward deposition pauses until the balance is cleared.

⚡ 3. Hash Cards & Cryptographic Synergy Exchanges

Participants can permanently scale their individual compute weight by exchanging randomly generated Hash Cards (e.g., Beetle Card, Spider Card, Rock Card, Satoshi Card) across community cryptographic channels, leveraging mathematical synergy multipliers for higher yields.

🎁 4. Single-Use Gift Card Matrix

New node runners can bind unique Gift Card codes to receive an immediate baseline computational jump (+30K Hash), while the issuing operator receives a perpetual micro-reward (+1K Hash) for onboarding genuine network participants.

Underlying Blockchain Foundation: Satoshi Plus Consensus

The integrity of tokens originating from the Satoshi platform is anchored directly in the Core DAO Layer-1 Blockchain. Core resolves the classic Blockchain Trilemma (achieving Decentralization, Security, and Scalability simultaneously) through its proprietary Satoshi Plus Consensus:

• Delegated Proof of Work (DPoW): Native Bitcoin (BTC) miners delegate their existing computational hashrate to Core validators without sacrificing electrical efficiency or altering Bitcoin mining routines, extending Bitcoin's fortress security to smart contracts.
• Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS): CORE token holders delegate staked capital to network validators, guaranteeing sub-second transaction finality, high throughput, and negligible transaction fees.
• Non-Custodial Bitcoin Staking: Core DAO pioneered trustless, absolute self-custody Bitcoin staking using absolute timelock cryptographic primitives on the native Bitcoin network, turning idle BTC into yield-generating collateral within the Core EVM ecosystem.

Sybil Resistance & Anti-Bot Infrastructure

Free airdrop distributions inherently attract industrial bot farms, multi-instance emulators, and automated script attacks. Satoshi App enforces rigorous anti-fraud measures to preserve reward value for legitimate users:

🛡️ Critical Security Rules & Audit Standards:

1. Biometric Facial Liveness Verification: The protocol requires each operator to pass an interactive 3D biometric liveness check. Static photo uploads, AI deepfakes, and automated scripts are blocked, ensuring an authenticated one-human, one-account environment.

2. Hardware & Emulator Restriction: Deploying the client across root-modified devices, cloud servers, or multi-instance PC emulators triggers immediate network isolation and permanent disqualification from pending airdrop contracts.

3. Zero Gas Fee Rule: Satoshi App never asks participants for upfront deposits, private keys, or wallet seed phrases to participate in daily round claims. All internal claiming operations are 100% free of charge.
