📖 Project Overview: What is Satoshi App?

Satoshi App is the official free airdrop and decentralized infrastructure application originated by the Core DAO community. It pioneered mobile-first crypto distribution by allowing millions of global users to participate in the early distribution of CORE tokens without requiring expensive ASIC rigs or electricity consumption.

Following the successful launch of the Core Mainnet in January 2023, Satoshi App transitioned into a premiere Multi-Project Free Airdrop Launchpad. Rather than mining standard Bitcoin or Core directly on mobile phones, users now participate in scheduled airdrop rounds from vetted Web3 partner protocols building on the Core Layer-1 chain.

💡
Current Operational Status: The original CORE mining phase concluded with the Mainnet genesis. Currently, Satoshi App operates as a zero-cost airdrop aggregator where users accumulate daily project tokens (such as OpenEX and ecosystem partner rewards) by maintaining their Hash power.

⚙️ Step-by-Step: How to Participate & Claim

  • 1

    Download via Official Portal

    Visit the official Satoshi website (btcs.fan) and install the official APK or Google Play release. Never install unofficial packages distributed via Telegram channels.

  • 2

    Complete I'm Not a Robot Facial Verification

    To prevent multi-account Sybil bot manipulation, the application requires a simple on-device biometric angle verification. No national identity card is required for basic app registration.

  • 3

    Select Active Airdrop Projects

    Navigate to the Projects tab inside the application and tap Participate in Airdrop on ongoing ecosystem tokens.

  • 4

    Claim Daily Airdrop Blocks (Every 24 Hours)

    Airdrop rounds generate automatically every hour. You must tap the Claim button at least once every 24 hours (before 25 rounds accumulate) to prevent automated round suspension.

⚡ Increasing Claim Velocity: HashCards & GiftCards

Your reward output in Satoshi App is proportional to your My Hash score relative to the total global hash pool:

• HashCards: Exchange card codes (Beetle Card, Ant Card, Spider Card, Rock Card, Satoshi Card) with fellow community members in verification groups to amplify your personal hashing rate by up to 120,000 Hash.
• GiftCards: Share 30K GiftCard codes with new community recruits to give them an instant starting boost while securing 1K permanent Hash for your node profile.

🛡️ Security & Anti-Scam Verification

Core DAO and Satoshi App will never ask for private keys, seed phrases, or gas fees to claim in-app airdrop rounds.

Always cross-reference smart contract addresses directly on the official CoreScan explorer. Avoid counterfeit Core or partner tokens traded on unauthorized decentralized exchanges.
