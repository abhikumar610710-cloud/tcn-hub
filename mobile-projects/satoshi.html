🌐 1. Overview

What Satoshi App Is: Satoshi App (originally launched during its test phase as Satoshi BTCs) is a community-focused Web3 airdrop distribution hub and zero-investment faucet model that helped distribute CORE, the native gas and governance asset of the Core DAO Layer-1 blockchain.

What Satoshi App Is Not: It is not a paid presale or a multi-emulator/bot farm—participation is designed around zero upfront capital, human liveness verification, and block-round claiming.

⚖️ Fair Distribution Model

Built to broaden community distribution rather than relying on private presales or whale-heavy allocations.

⛓️ Core DAO Anchor

Connected to the Core DAO Layer-1 ecosystem secured by Satoshi Plus consensus (DPoW, DPoS, and Bitcoin Staking).

⚙️ 2. How It Works

Satoshi App coordinates hourly block rounds, claim windows, and community hash boosts across active campaigns:

⚙️ Hourly Block Rounds

Participating airdrop campaigns release reward allocations across sequential consensus block rounds.

⏱️ 25-Round Suspension

Unclaimed rounds pause after 25 rounds (roughly 24 hours) until you claim your accumulated blocks.

⚡ Hash Card Synergy

Exchange randomly generated Hash Cards with other participants to boost your compute weight during active campaigns.

🎁 Gift Card Boost

Eligible new accounts can apply Gift Card codes (such as +30K Hash bonuses where supported) to raise initial hash power.

🛡️ 4. Safety & Anti-Bot Compliance

Verification controls and security practices for Satoshi App users:

Biometric Liveness Interactive facial liveness checks enforce a one-human, one-account rule against bots and impersonation.
No Emulators or Rooted Devices Avoid multi-instance PC emulators, rooted phones, or cloud servers that risk account suspension.
Zero Fee & Key Hygiene Claiming rounds requires no upfront fee; never share passwords, private keys, or seed phrases.

⚡ 5. Earning, Claims & Mainnet Flow

How campaign points move from in-app rounds to on-chain wallets:

Pre-TGE Allocation Tracking In-app balances track campaign participation prior to each project's Token Generation Event (TGE).
EVM Wallet Binding Link a non-custodial EVM wallet (such as MetaMask or Core Wallet) ahead of distribution windows.
Smart Contract Claims Eligible tokens are claimed via official project portals onto supported mainnets and traded on DEX/CEX markets.

❓ 9. FAQ

What is Satoshi App?

Satoshi App is a Web3 airdrop distribution hub and Core DAO genesis distribution network that distributes token allocations across block rounds.

Is there a fee to claim airdrop rounds?

No, claiming daily airdrop rounds inside Satoshi App requires zero upfront payment; only standard network gas applies when claiming on-chain tokens.

What happens if I miss the 25-round window?

If 25 rounds (~24 hours) pass without claiming, automated round accumulation pauses until you claim the pending rounds.

How do Hash Cards and Gift Cards work?

Hash Cards are exchanged with other users to boost compute weight during campaigns, while Gift Cards can provide an initial hash boost for eligible new accounts.
