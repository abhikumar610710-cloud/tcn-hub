What is Holana Network? (Origins & Polygon PoS Framework)

Holana Network (HOL) is a mobile-first cryptocurrency distribution protocol deployed on the Polygon PoS (ERC-20) blockchain. Designed to remove technical barriers to cryptocurrency onboarding, Holana replaces computationally intensive proof-of-work mining with a cloud-validated tap consensus framework.

Unlike traditional mining projects that operate on closed testnets, Holana's native token contract is fully live on Polygon. Users participate through either the official Android application or directly through a zero-install web browser interface, linking their Google identity to begin collecting points with zero device battery drain or hardware wear.

Mining Architecture: 24-Hour Cycle & 10:1 Conversion Ratio

Holana operates on a mathematical points-to-token redemption pipeline:

⚡ 1. Daily 24-Hour Check-In Session

Participants initialize an active production cycle once every 24 hours with a single tap. The session runs continuously in the cloud, generating Holana Points (HP) based on active participation without requiring the browser or app to stay open.

🔄 2. Fixed 10:1 HP to HOL Redemption

Accumulated Holana Points convert into native on-chain tokens at a fixed mathematical ratio of 10 HP = 1 HOL. This formula eliminates arbitrary exchange rate manipulation and provides absolute accounting clarity.

🌐 3. Multi-Platform Browser Accessibility

Mining sessions and reward claims can be executed seamlessly across mobile phones, tablets, or desktop browsers using unified Google account authentication.

💼 4. Direct Self-Custody Polygonscan Withdrawals

Converted $HOL balances migrate directly to self-custody Web3 wallets (MetaMask, Bitget Wallet, Trust Wallet), with every transaction verifiable in real time on Polygonscan.
