📱 1. Overview

What Alpha Network Is: Operating via minealpha.net and mobile applications since 2021, Alpha Network is a mobile check-in and reward app where users start 24-hour sessions, claim rewards, stake balances, and participate in in-app auctions without running heavy local hardware.

What Alpha Network Is Not: It is not a real proof-of-work hardware miner or physical DePIN sensor network—balances, auction settlement, and liquidity depend on the platform's internal app mechanics and ecosystem rules.

⏱️ 24-Hour Session Model

Users tap once every 24 hours to keep their active session running on server-side cloud tracking with minimal phone battery impact.

⚖️ Realistic Liquidity Context

User reviews across mobile app ecosystems have frequently raised questions around ad frequency and swap/liquidity pace—approach with measured expectations.

🛡️ 4. Safety, Security & Risk Considerations

Key practical precautions when using mobile reward apps and name-colliding crypto projects:

Name Collision Warning Several unrelated projects use "Alpha Network" or "$ALPHA" (such as alphacoin.network or alphanetx.xyz). Always verify you are on minealpha.net or the intended project.
Reward & Liquidity Reality In-app balances and auction mechanics do not guarantee fixed cash value, instant liquidity, or external exchange listings.
One-Account Integrity Avoid multi-account emulators or policy violations that can restrict account access, auction eligibility, or withdrawals.
Seed Phrase & Key Safety Never share your 12/24-word recovery phrase or private keys with anyone claiming to offer support or faster withdrawals.

❓ 9. FAQ

Does Alpha Network mine crypto using my phone's CPU or GPU?

No. Alpha Network uses a 24-hour app check-in and server-tracked reward model rather than running hardware hashpower or heavy local mining on your smartphone.

What are Alpha Auctions and in-app BNB features?

They are platform-specific features inside Alpha Network that facilitate bidding pools/auctions and BNB withdrawal or wallet interactions subject to app rules.

Why are there different "Alpha Network" crypto projects?

Multiple unrelated Web3 projects share similar names or tickers. Always verify the exact domain (such as minealpha.net) and official documentation before interacting.

Do I need to pay money upfront to join?

No upfront capital investment is required to create an account and start standard 24-hour mobile check-in sessions.
