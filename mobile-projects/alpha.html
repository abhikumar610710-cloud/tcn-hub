What is Alpha Network? (Origins, Community Scale & Architecture)

Alpha Network is an established decentralized mobile cryptocurrency protocol launched in late 2020 to introduce mainstream smartphone users to cloud-based digital asset mining[cite: 53, 55]. Built with an emphasis on simplicity, security, and low device overhead ("Fast, Safe, Easy"), the network has scaled its global community to over 2,000,000+ registered members[cite: 53, 55].

Operating under the mission of building a fair and decentralized mobile currency ledger, Alpha eliminates the need for expensive proof-of-work graphic processors or battery-intensive background tasks[cite: 53, 54]. Instead, the system leverages authenticated cloud epochs to coordinate verified user check-ins and gradual token issuance directly across iOS and Android ecosystems.
