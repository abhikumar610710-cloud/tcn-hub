What is Pi Network? (Origins & Layer 1 Framework)

Pi Network is an independent Layer-1 cryptocurrency protocol launched on March 14, 2019 (Pi Day) by Stanford PhD graduates Dr. Nicolas Kokkalis and Dr. Chengdiao Fan under SocialChain Inc. It was developed to explore a widely accessible alternative to traditional Proof-of-Work (PoW) mining models like Bitcoin, which rely on specialized ASIC hardware and high electrical overhead.

Instead of running intensive hashing algorithms on device hardware, Pi uses a server-coordinated check-in system where registered users confirm activity once every 24 hours. The mobile client acts as an identity and participation portal rather than a cryptographic hashing engine.

How Mobile Mining Works: Daily Check-In & Security Circles

Pi's smartphone participation model eliminates hardware wear and focuses strictly on verifiable human presence:

⚡ 1. Daily 24-Hour Tap-to-Mine Cycle

Users initiate their mining session by tapping the lightning button in the mobile app once every 24 hours. This action proves daily active presence on the network and maintains continuous base-rate point accumulation without keeping the screen active.

🛡️ 2. Security Circle Boost (100% Rate Optimization)

Mining rates scale when users establish a Security Circle with 3 to 5 trusted members. This trust graph verifies individual connection authenticity, protecting the network from bot manipulation while providing the user with maximum baseline mining rewards.

🔑 3. Self-Custody Pi Browser & 24-Word Passphrase

Accumulated tokens migrate directly to a non-custodial wallet created within the official Pi Browser. Users hold complete ownership of their account assets through a private 24-word Passphrase.
