📖 Project Overview: What is Pi Network?

Pi Network is a social cryptocurrency and developer platform founded by Stanford PhDs Dr. Nicolas Kokkalis and Dr. Chengdiao Fan. It introduced mobile-based mining to the global mainstream by utilizing a consensus algorithm adapted from the Stellar Consensus Protocol (SCP), allowing everyday smartphone users to mine native PI coins without battery depletion, hardware damage, or electrical costs.

Unlike traditional Proof-of-Work (PoW) networks, Pi secures its distributed ledger through user-validated Security Circles—interlocking webs of trusted connections that establish trust graphs and validate transaction integrity across millions of global community members (Pioneers).

Listing Reality & IOU Clarification: Pi Network is currently operating within an Enclosed Mainnet protected by an institutional firewall. Pi has NOT authorized any public exchange listings. 🚀 Enclosed Mainnet to Open Mainnet Criteria

The Pi Core Team has established concrete milestone conditions required to dismantle the external firewall and transition into Open Mainnet:

• KYC Thresholds: Millions of verified Pioneer identity clearances across global jurisdictions to guarantee real-human decentralization.
• Mainnet Migration: Transferring verified balances into self-custodial on-chain wallets on the Pi Mainnet blockchain.
• Utility Acceleration: Fostering live decentralized applications (DApps) accepting Pi for real-world peer-to-peer goods and services.

1. Never enter your 24-word Passphrase on external websites: The Pi Core Team will never request your secret seed phrase. Only enter your passphrase directly inside the official wallet.pi URL inside the verified Pi Browser.
