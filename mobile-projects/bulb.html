What is BULB? (Web3 Do-to-Earn Blogging Platform)

BULB is a revolutionary Web3 blogging and content creation platform built on the high-speed Solana blockchain. Unlike traditional Web2 blogging platforms that monetize user data and retain 100% of ad revenues, BULB operates on a "Do-to-Earn" (D2E) engagement model where 100% of the community-generated value flows directly back to writers and readers.

Founded with the vision to eliminate central intermediaries, data exploitation, and algorithmic bias, BULB empowers creators, readers, and crypto enthusiasts to share ideas, publish articles, and earn tradeable crypto assets based on authentic engagement.

Write-to-Earn (W2E) & Read-to-Earn (R2E) Mechanics

✍️ Write-to-Earn (W2E)

Publish quality articles, guides, and thoughts. Collect BULB points whenever community members read, react, share, or comment on your posts.

📖 Read-to-Earn (R2E)

Get rewarded simply for reading articles, leaving insightful comments, reacting to posts, and engaging with content creators.

⚡ Daily Energy System

Every account has a daily Energy balance. Spending Energy on reading, writing, and reacting converts your effort into leaderboard points.

🛡️ Equal Level Playing Field

BULB's transparent algorithm highlights both established authors and new undiscovered writers with fresh perspectives.

Understanding BULB Energy, Points & BULBmoji NFTs

BULB Energy Refills automatically every 24 hours. Spent when publishing articles, commenting, or reacting to posts.
BULB Points Earned instantly through all platform activities. Points determine your rank in the weekly reward distribution pool.
BULBmoji NFTs Collectible utility NFTs that boost point multipliers, unlock exclusive emojis, and increase daily Energy capacity.

$BULB Tokenomics & Solana Integration

The $BULB Token is the native SPL token powering the entire platform ecosystem on Solana. With a total capped supply of 1,000,000,000 (1 Billion) $BULB, the token supply is divided equally between the community and core platform operations.

$BULB follows a deflationary model through token burns, post boosting mechanisms, and premium content unlocks. $BULB tokens can be traded on decentralized exchanges (DEXs) such as Raydium, Jupiter, and Orca.
