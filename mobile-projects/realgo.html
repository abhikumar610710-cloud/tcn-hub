Protocol Overview & Dynamic LBS Architecture

RealGo transforms the physical world into an interactive Augmented Reality (AR) and Location-Based Services (LBS) playground. Combining modern smartphone GPS tracking with on-chain Web3 mechanics, players explore their local surroundings to discover, capture, battle, and upgrade over 120 unique meme-inspired pets including legendary icons like Doge, Pepe, and Shiba.

Through its Dynamic Map Mechanic, rare meme characters and resource nodes spawn at specific geo-coordinates and intervals. Users utilize specialized capture tools (like Elite Orbs) to collect companions, level up their skill sets using Skill Tomes, and construct specialized meme combat squads.
