What is TTcoin? (Origins, TSC Smart Chain & Governance)

TTcoin (TC) was founded on March 24, 2021, and operates under the corporate governance of TTcoin Joint Stock Company[cite: 9, 11]. Built to establish an accessible digital value ecosystem, TTcoin has expanded to over 20 million community users and more than 419,000 registered non-custodial wallets globally[cite: 6].

Rather than functioning solely as a third-party token, TTcoin operates its own independent Layer-1 smart contract infrastructure—TTcoin Smart Chain (TSC)[cite: 8]. To maximize cross-chain liquidity and decentralized finance interoperability, TC is also bridged and traded across BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20) and Tron Network (TRC-20).

How Mobile Mining Works: Daily Sessions & Cloud Node Rewards

TTcoin enables everyday smartphone users to participate in ecosystem token issuance through a zero-hardware mobile mining model:

⚡ 1. 6-Hour / 24-Hour Active Cycle Check-In

Participants activate their mobile session directly inside the official TTcoin Network app. The cloud server credits verified TC balances based on active session intervals without requiring device hardware or CPU computation.

🛡️ 2. Multi-Tier Reward Infrastructure

Session yield is allocated directly from the predefined Mining Reserve (2,716,299,669 TC), ensuring fixed distribution limits that prevent arbitrary algorithmic token inflation[cite: 9].

💼 3. TC-Wallet Pro Integration

Earned balances are migrated to TC-Wallet Pro, the native Web3 mobile wallet configured for the TSC Smart Chain, allowing secure local private key custody and decentralized transaction execution[cite: 7].
