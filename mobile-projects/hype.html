What is Hype Network? (Origins, Vision & Architecture)

Hype Network is an advanced mobile crypto mining and Web3 ecosystem application designed to make digital asset accumulation seamless for Android users. Built around the core mechanism "Mine HYPED every 6 hours," the platform enables users to run continuous automated production sessions that accumulate tokens even when the app is closed[cite: 10].

The project is structured with a fixed maximum supply of 10 billion tokens and aims to expand into a robust Web3 ecosystem featuring a peer-to-peer marketplace and DAO governance[cite: 11, 13].
