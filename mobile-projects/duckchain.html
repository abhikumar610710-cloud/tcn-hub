🌐 1. Overview

What DuckChain Is: DuckChain is a consumer Layer-2 blockchain protocol designed to connect the TON (Telegram Open Network) ecosystem with the Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM) environment using Arbitrum Orbit scaling technology.

What DuckChain Is Not: It is not a passive bandwidth-sharing browser extension or a desktop CPU/GPU mining node. Its consumer engagement has centered around Telegram Mini-App campaigns (such as Egg Season), EVM wallet binding, and Layer-2 smart contract utility.

🌉 TON ↔ EVM Bridge

Enables Telegram users to interact with EVM-compatible DeFi, gaming dApps, and cross-chain liquidity without leaving the Telegram experience.

⚡ Gas Abstraction

Supports flexible gas mechanics (including Telegram Stars, TON, and USDT abstraction concepts) to lower onboarding friction for everyday users.

⚙️ 2. How It Works

DuckChain combines Layer-2 rollup execution with gamified community participation and wallet binding:

Arbitrum Orbit Execution

Built on the Arbitrum Orbit Layer-2 framework to process fast transactions and settle batch proofs securely toward Ethereum/Arbitrum layers.

Egg Season & Quests

Community members participated in daily egg cycles, partner quests, and Telegram Mini-App interactions to build campaign points.

Gas Abstraction Layer

Designed to let users pay or abstract transaction gas smoothly across familiar assets like Telegram Stars, TON, and USDT.

EVM & CEX Reward Binding

Users connect an EVM wallet (such as OKX Wallet or MetaMask) or enter a supported exchange deposit address/Memo for reward distribution.

🛡️ 4. Safety

Follow these essential anti-Sybil and wallet security practices when interacting with DuckChain:

Anti-Sybil Filtering Multi-account bots and cloud emulators can be filtered out by identity and behavioral checks; keep participation genuine and organic.
Private Keys & Seed Phrases Never share your wallet recovery phrase or private keys with any bot, support DM, or third-party website.
Memo / UID Accuracy If choosing a centralized exchange deposit address instead of a self-custody EVM wallet, double-check both the deposit address and required Memo/UID.
