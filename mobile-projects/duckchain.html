What is DuckChain? (TON EVM Layer-2)

DuckChain is an innovative consumer Layer-2 blockchain protocol built directly to connect the TON (Telegram Open Network) ecosystem with the global Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM) landscape.

By allowing Telegram's massive user base of over 900 million users to interact with EVM-compatible decentralized finance (DeFi), gaming dApps, and non-fungible digital assets without switching networks, DuckChain bridges the liquidity gap between Ethereum, Arbitrum, and Telegram.
