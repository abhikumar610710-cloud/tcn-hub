What is Omega Network? (Origins & Layer 1 Framework)

Omega Network (OMN / OMG) is an independent Layer-1 blockchain platform engineered to provide a decentralized alternative to computationally heavy Proof-of-Work protocols[cite: 6, 7]. Originating as a smartphone-accessible consensus project, Omega Network operates an EVM-compatible chain designed for everyday participation through a human-verified Proof-of-Presence mechanism[cite: 6, 7].

Unlike traditional crypto networks that require industrial ASIC hardware or specialized high-voltage electricity lines, Omega coordinates consensus sessions directly through an energy-efficient mobile interface[cite: 6, 7]. Participants initialize a 24-hour mining cycle with a single check-in, enabling secure token issuance without draining local device memory or processing power.
