About Us

Welcome to Total Crypto Hub, an open tracking and educational directory curated by the Total Crypto Network community. Our goal is to provide verified, objective, and timely insights into Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks (DePIN), edge computing nodes, bandwidth monetization, and decentralized hardware networks.

Our Mission

The decentralized internet is evolving rapidly, making it difficult for node runners and Web3 participants to identify legitimate infrastructure projects. Total Crypto Hub bridges this gap by cataloging active networks, verifying their token listing status, and highlighting direct cash payout mechanisms to empower individuals globally.

What We Track

  • Verified DePIN Protocols: Networks leveraging decentralized compute, storage, bandwidth, and environmental sensors.
  • Direct Payout Infrastructure: Established passive monetization platforms that offer direct USD or stable payout solutions.
  • Active Testnets & Nodes: Early-stage decentralized networks developing distributed infrastructure.

Independent & Community Driven

Total Crypto Hub operates as an educational resource. All project metrics, status tags, and score calculations are compiled to assist users in understanding node operations, software requirements, and network mechanics before deploying hardware.
