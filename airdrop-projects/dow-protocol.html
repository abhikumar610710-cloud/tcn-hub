What is Dow Protocol? (E-Commerce Supply Chain RWA Platform)

Dow Protocol (accessible at app.dowprotocol.com) is a decentralized E-Commerce Financing & Supply Chain Real-World Asset (RWA) Platform engineered to connect on-chain liquidity with real-world online merchant financing. Global e-commerce merchants selling across major marketplaces frequently require fast working capital to bridge inventory cycles and settlement delays.

Through structured RWA liquidity vaults—such as Dowsure Stable #1 on Volo—Dow Protocol channels capital into Direct Payment Loan (DPL) and Line of Credit (LOC) products for large-scale e-commerce merchants and service providers, backed by verifiable receivable records.

Alongside its yield vaults and portfolio dashboard, Dow Protocol operates a zero-cost XP Points Campaign where community members can connect social accounts, verify community posts, and accumulate XP ahead of future ecosystem milestones.

Step-by-Step XP Points & Quest Breakdown

Users can participate in the free Points Campaign directly at app.dowprotocol.com/points/my by completing four sequential tasks worth +200 XP:

1️⃣ Connect X (+50 XP)

Link your active X (Twitter) profile to your Dow Protocol account to establish your social identity and claim your initial +50 XP.

2️⃣ Share DOW on X (+50 XP)

Click Post now to publish the official Dow Protocol post on X, copy your live post URL (https://x.com/username/status/...), and submit it for review.

3️⃣ Invite a Friend (+50 XP)

Once your X post passes the 24-hour review, the Invite a friend quest unlocks—generating your personal referral link to earn +50 XP per invite.

4️⃣ Connect Telegram (+50 XP)

After your X verification completes, connect your Telegram handle to join the official community and earn an extra +50 XP.

Mandatory Verification & RWA Risk Guidelines

To protect your XP balance and ensure safe interaction with on-chain RWA vaults, follow these mandatory protocol guidelines:

⚠️ Mandatory XP & Protocol Rules:

1. Keep Your X Post Live Permanently: Post verification takes up to 24 hours before crediting +50 XP. Deleting your X post after submission can cause your associated XP to be revoked.

2. Do Not Change Your X Handle: Keep your X username unchanged during and after review so automated and manual checks can continuously validate your account.

3. Review RWA Vault Terms Before Depositing: Before committing capital into yield vaults like Dowsure Stable #1, inspect the Merchant Risk Analysis data, delinquency metrics, and official smart-contract Audit documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Dowsure Stable #1 vault on Dow Protocol? +
Deployed in partnership with Volo, Dowsure Stable #1 is an RWA liquidity vault that funds Direct Payment Loan (DPL) and Line of Credit (LOC) products for large-scale e-commerce merchants, displaying $501,300 in volume and a 19.00% Vault APY.
Why is "Invite a friend" locked when I first join Dow Protocol? +
Dow Protocol requires users to connect their X account and submit a live X post sharing DOW first. Once your post passes verification (which takes up to 24 hours), the Invite a friend and Connect Telegram quests unlock.
What is shown in the Merchant Risk Analysis section? +
The Risk Analysis tab displays Season 1 and Season 2 receivable-level records, including unique Receivable IDs, marketplace Store IDs, store performance metrics, and settlement histories.
Can I delete my X post after receiving the +50 XP? +
No. Dow Protocol states that you must keep your X post live and keep your X username unchanged—deleting the post may cause your credited XP to be revoked.
Do I need to deposit funds to earn XP Points? +
No. The social quests in the Points tab (Connect X, Share DOW on X, Invite a friend, and Connect Telegram) are completely free to complete without staking funds in the RWA vaults.
