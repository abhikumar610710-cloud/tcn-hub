🌐 1. Overview (E-Commerce Supply Chain RWAFi Platform)

What Dow Protocol Is: Dow Protocol (accessible at app.dowprotocol.com) is a decentralized On-Chain E-Commerce Financing & Supply Chain RWAFi (Real-World Asset + PayFi) Protocol engineered to connect on-chain liquidity with real-world online merchant working capital. Global e-commerce merchants selling across major marketplaces frequently wait weeks for marketplace payouts; Dow Protocol advances instant stablecoin liquidity backed by verified receivables and automated settlement.

Live RWA Vaults & XP Campaign: Through structured RWA vaults—such as Dowsure Stable #1 on Volo—Dow Protocol funds Direct Payment Loan (DPL) and Line of Credit (LOC) products for large-scale e-commerce merchants. Alongside its yield vaults, it operates a zero-cost DOW XP & AI Quests Campaign for early community contributors.

What Dow Protocol Is Not: It is not risk-free bank interest or a confirmed token airdrop; RWA vaults carry smart-contract and merchant credit exposure, while free social quests reward DOW XP ahead of future ecosystem milestones.

To protect your XP balance and ensure safe interaction with on-chain RWA vaults, follow these mandatory protocol guidelines:

1. Keep Your X Post Live Permanently Post verification takes up to 24 hours before crediting +50 XP. Deleting your X post after submission can cause your associated XP to be revoked.
2. Do Not Change Your X Handle Keep your X username unchanged during and after review so automated and manual checks can continuously validate your account.

❓ 9. FAQ

What is the Dowsure Stable #1 vault on Dow Protocol?

Deployed in partnership with Volo, Dowsure Stable #1 is an RWA liquidity vault that funds Direct Payment Loan (DPL) and Line of Credit (LOC) products for large-scale e-commerce merchants, displaying $501,300 in volume and a target 19.00% Vault APY.

Why is "Invite a friend" locked when I first join Dow Protocol?

Dow Protocol requires users to connect their X account and submit a live X post sharing DOW first. Once your post passes verification (which takes up to 24 hours), the Invite a friend and Connect Telegram quests unlock.

What is shown in the Merchant Risk Analysis section?

The Risk Analysis tab displays Season 1 and Season 2 receivable-level records, including unique Receivable IDs, marketplace Store IDs, store performance metrics, and settlement histories.

Can I delete my X post after receiving the +50 XP?

No. Dow Protocol states that you must keep your X post live and keep your X username unchanged—deleting the post may cause your credited XP to be revoked.

Do I need to deposit funds to earn DOW XP Points?

No. The social quests, daily AI tasks, and quizzes in the Points tab (Connect X, Share DOW on X, Invite a friend, and Connect Telegram) are completely free to complete without staking funds in the RWA vaults.
