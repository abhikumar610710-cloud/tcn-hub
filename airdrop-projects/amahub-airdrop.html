🌐 1. Overview (What AMA Hub Is, Why It Matters & Core Value)

What AMA Hub Is: AMA Hub (amahub.ama.one) is the user-facing AI agent and community quest hub built around Amadeus Protocol (ama.one), an AI-native Layer-1 network designed for private, deterministic AI agents and agentic finance.

Why It Stands Out: Instead of forcing everyday users to run complex servers or virtual machines (VMs) just to participate in early ecosystem activity, AMA Hub combines daily check-ins, social/community quests, and agent/publishing tasks with PRIME Points and defined weekly claim windows.

🤖 AI-Native Layer-1 & Agents

Built on Amadeus Protocol's high-speed architecture with sub-second finality, TEE confidential compute, and integrated AI agent tooling (including the February 2026 acquisition of Bitte.ai).

🎯 VM-Free Quests & Community Events

Supports VM-free quest routes, daily check-ins, social actions, and community campaigns so users can participate directly from their browser and wallet.

⚙️ 2. How It Works (Wallets, Daily Streaks, Quests & Weekly Claims)

AMA Hub connects wallet identity with daily engagement, quest completion, and weekly reward claims:

🔗 Step 1: Connect EVM & AMA Wallet

Connect and link your supported EVM wallet and AMA Wallet (browser extension, web, or mobile) to register your profile and track quest progress.

📅 Step 2: Maintain Daily Check-In Streaks

Visit the hub daily and complete your check-in to collect daily points (such as +25 PRIME Points/day) and keep your streak active.

🧩 Step 3: Complete Social, Referral & Agent Quests

Finish X/social actions, Discord/Telegram tasks, referral milestones, and agent/publishing quests—using VM-free options where supported.

⏱️ Step 4: Claim Within Weekly 7-Day Windows

Eligible quest rewards release on a weekly schedule and must be claimed within their 7-day claim window on your dashboard.

🛡️ 4. Safety Rules, Claim Windows, Cost vs. Fees & Wallet Security

Key security practices, claim rules, and cost expectations for AMA Hub users:

1. Weekly 7-Day Claim Window Weekly quest rewards have a 7-day claim window once released—check your dashboard regularly so you do not miss active claims.
2. Free Quests vs. Optional On-Chain / Trading Daily check-ins and basic social quests are free, whereas optional trading or on-chain/agent actions may involve network/trading fees or capital risk.
3. Dedicated Secondary Wallet Always use a dedicated secondary/burner wallet for Web3 quests and never share private keys or 12/24-word seed phrases.
4. Official Domain & Anti-Phishing Check Only interact with official URLs (amahub.ama.one and ama.one) and ignore fake claim/airdrop links in replies or DMs.

❓ 9. FAQ

Do I need a referral code or virtual machine (VM) to use AMA Hub?

No—you can visit https://amahub.ama.one directly, and many quests (including daily check-ins, social tasks, and supported agent/publishing routes) offer VM-free participation.

What are PRIME Points and how do I earn them?

PRIME Points are earned through daily check-ins, social/community tasks, referrals, and hub quests. Any future conversion or reward terms are governed by Amadeus Protocol.

How long is the weekly claim window for quest rewards?

Released weekly quest rewards have a 7-day claim window, so check your dashboard each week to claim on time.

Where can I check $AMA tokenomics, litepaper, and funding details?

The Amadeus Litepaper specifies a hard cap of 1,000,000,000 (1 Billion) $AMA distributed via Useful Proof of Work (uPoW); visit https://www.ama.one for official documentation and updates.

Is trading required to complete AMA Hub quests?

No. Daily check-ins and social quests do not require trading; any trading or on-chain activity is optional and carries its own costs and risks.
