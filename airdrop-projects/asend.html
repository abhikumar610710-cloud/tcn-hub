What is Asend? (Trade. Share. Asend.)

Asend (available at asend.now) is a next-generation mobile-first social crypto trading platform designed to simplify on-chain token trading and community sharing. Traditional decentralized exchanges (DEXs) often create friction for everyday users by requiring separate native gas tokens (such as BNB), complex wallet approvals, and manual chart screenshots when sharing market insights.

Asend combines seamless gas-free execution with built-in social discovery. Users can buy and sell supported tokens directly from a clean mobile interface without needing to hold extra BNB for network fees, while turning live trades into verified, shareable position cards for social platforms.

Whether you are exploring early ecosystem apps, tracking market sentiment through community threads, or sharing verified trade results on X (Twitter), Instagram, TikTok, or Threads, Asend streamlines both execution and social engagement inside a single app.
