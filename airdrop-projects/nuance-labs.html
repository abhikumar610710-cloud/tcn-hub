🌐 1. Overview (Real-Time Audio-Visual AI & Open Waitlist)

What Nuance Labs Is: Nuance Labs (nuancelabs.ai) is an artificial intelligence research organization based in Seattle, Washington, focused on real-time audio-visual AI models and interactive conversational intelligence.

Unified Pipeline vs. Multi-Stage Chains: Rather than chaining separate Speech-to-Text (STT), Large Language Model (LLM), Text-to-Speech (TTS), and visual rendering steps that add latency, Nuance Labs focuses on unified real-time audio-visual interaction and early beta onboarding via its public waitlist.
