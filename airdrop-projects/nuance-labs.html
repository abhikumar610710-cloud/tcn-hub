Protocol Overview & Real-Time Multimodal Architecture

Nuance Labs is a frontier artificial intelligence research organization headquartered in Seattle, Washington, building the foundational emotional and audio-visual intelligence layer for next-generation interactive AI systems.

Current AI avatars and voice agents rely on clunky, multi-stage pipelines: Speech-to-Text (STT) followed by Large Language Model (LLM) processing, followed by Text-to-Speech (TTS) and video rendering. This introduces massive latency and breaks emotional synchrony. Nuance Labs is engineering the first unified audio-visual foundation model that operates as a single neural pipeline—seeing, hearing, reasoning, speaking, and expressing emotion simultaneously in ultra-low latency real time.

Unified Audio-Visual Neural Net
