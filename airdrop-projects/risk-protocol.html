RiskON vs. RiskOFF Prediction Market Mechanics

- RiskON Option: Selected when you predict Bitcoin or Ethereum prices will rise or meet target market benchmarks within the round timeframe.
- RiskOFF Option: Selected when you predict market consolidation or price pullbacks.
- Real-Time Community Consensus: The dashboard displays live community sentiment percentages (e.g., RiskON 67.28% vs RiskOFF) to help users analyze trends before committing test assets.
