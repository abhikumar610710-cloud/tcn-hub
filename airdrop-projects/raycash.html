Protocol Overview & Confidential Neobanking Architecture

Raycash is a self-custodial mobile money account and consumer fintech application engineered to integrate digital stablecoins (USDC/USDT) into daily real-world payments. Spun out of the advanced cryptography pioneer Zama, Raycash bridges the divide between non-custodial decentralized finance (DeFi) and compliant everyday banking.

On public blockchains like Ethereum and Polygon, account balances and payment transactions are completely exposed to public surveillance. Raycash resolves this fundamental privacy flaw using Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE). Standard stablecoins are programmatically converted into confidential wrappers, allowing smart contracts to compute payments and settle balances while retaining total encryption on-chain.
