🏛️ 1. Overview

Bybit is a centralized cryptocurrency exchange (CEX) that offers digital-asset trading and related services. Its product categories include Spot trading, derivatives, options, Copy Trading, trading bots, Earn products, and P2P services. Bybit also describes a Unified Trading Account (UTA) that provides access to supported trading products through a unified account structure.

Beginners should start by understanding Spot orders, fees, and account security—derivatives, margin, and options can magnify losses and are not beginner shortcuts. Availability and verification requirements depend on your country and account.

Potential Advantages

  • Multi-Module Trading & Learning: Access to multiple trading product categories, Spot markets and live market data, Unified Trading Account features, plus Bybit Learn and Help Center resources.

Things to Consider

  • Custody, Fees & Leverage Risk: Exchange custody and platform risk, maker/taker fees, spreads and funding costs, regional product differences, and rapid liquidation risk on leverage.

This is a dated company announcement, not a guarantee that every product, payment method, or account feature is available now. ⭐ 2. Key Features

Bybit provides Spot, derivatives, automated strategy, and passive savings modules inside its platform:

  • Spot Trading: Buy or sell supported crypto pairs using available order types—review the order book, quote, and fee before placing an order.
  • Derivatives & Options: Explore eligible contracts only after learning margin, funding, expiry, and liquidation risks; access varies by jurisdiction.
  • Copy Trading: Eligible users may follow selected traders—past performance does not predict future results, and losses remain possible.
  • Bybit Earn: Review eligible savings and on-chain yield products, lock-up terms, and asset risks carefully before subscribing.

🗝️ 5. Custody & Control

An exchange account on Bybit is custodial and differs from a self-custody wallet. Assets held in an exchange account are subject to the platform's custody, account, and withdrawal arrangements.

  • Bybit Exchange vs. Bybit Wallet: Bybit Exchange holds assets under platform account custody, whereas the separate Bybit Wallet product has different self-custody and access characteristics—always verify which product you are using.
  • Regional & Verification Terms: Account verification (KYC), deposit/withdrawal limits, and country-specific restrictions govern how funds are accessed and withdrawn.

❓ 10. FAQ

1. Is Bybit a wallet or an exchange?

What it is: This page covers Bybit Exchange, a centralized exchange (CEX). The separate Bybit Wallet product has different custody and access characteristics.

How to stay safe: Always check whether you are interacting with your custodial exchange account or a self-custody Web3 wallet.

2. Does Bybit require identity verification?

What to know: Verification requirements depend on the service, account tier, and jurisdiction.

How to check: Bybit may direct users to a regional platform or show a country-specific notice during registration.

3. What is a Unified Trading Account (UTA)?

What it is: Bybit's Unified Trading Account is designed to provide access to supported Spot, margin, perpetual, futures, and options products within a unified account structure.

How to check: Collateral, cross-margin rules, and regional product eligibility still apply.

4. Are Bybit fees always the same?

What to know: No—fees depend on product category, maker/taker role, VIP level, 30-day trading volume, and region, and funding or spread costs may also apply.

How to check: Review the current fee schedule at bybit.com/en/fee before trading.

5. Is Copy Trading guaranteed to make profit?

What to know: No—Copy Trading can lose money, and a lead trader's past results do not guarantee future performance.

How to stay safe: Review strategy details, risk settings, profit-sharing fees, and maximum drawdowns before allocating funds.

6. Are the BTC, ETH, and XRP prices on this page live?

What it is: The market cards request public Bybit V5 Spot ticker snapshots and refresh about every 30 seconds.

How to check: They are indicative quotes in USDT rather than guaranteed tick-by-tick order-book prices; check Bybit Markets for live trading screens.
