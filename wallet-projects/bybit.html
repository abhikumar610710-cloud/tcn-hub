1 What is Bybit?

Bybit is a centralized cryptocurrency exchange (CEX) that offers digital-asset trading and related services. Its product categories include Spot trading, derivatives, options, Copy Trading, trading bots, Earn products and P2P services. Bybit also describes a Unified Trading Account (UTA) that can provide access to supported trading products through a unified account structure.

An exchange account is different from a self-custody wallet. Assets held in an exchange account are subject to the platform's custody, account and withdrawal arrangements. Review the terms, supported networks, fees and risks before depositing or trading.

💡

Beginner Note

5 Account Security Tips

  • 🛡️Use Bybit Protect. Review the security recommendations shown for your account and enable available protection levels.
  • 🔐Enable 2FA or a passkey. Secure your email and device as well as your Bybit account.
  • 📩Set an anti-phishing code. Check the code in genuine messages and verify links before signing in.
  • 📍Review withdrawal safeguards. Use available address-book, withdrawal-lock or location-based protections where appropriate.
  • ✋Never share codes or secrets. Do not disclose passwords, OTPs, 2FA codes, API secrets or remote-access permissions to anyone.
