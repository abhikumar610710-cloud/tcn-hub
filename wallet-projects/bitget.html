🛡️ 1. Overview

Bitget Wallet is a non-custodial, multi-chain cryptocurrency wallet available on iOS, Android, and as a Chrome browser extension. It provides tools to manage supported digital assets, connect to decentralized applications (DApps), swap tokens, and move assets across supported EVM and non-EVM networks.

Self-custody means you are responsible for protecting your wallet access and approving transactions. Your assets are recorded on their respective blockchains; the wallet is the interface used to manage access. Never assume that a token, DApp, bridge, or transaction is safe just because it appears inside a wallet.

What It Helps You Do

  • Multi-Chain & DApp Access: Manage multi-chain assets, perform token swaps and cross-chain routes, discover DApps, and access supported staking/earn or payment services.

What to Keep in Mind

  • Regional Eligibility & Irreversibility: Availability of cards, fiat on/off-ramps, and earning products varies by country and product terms; on-chain transactions are generally irreversible.
Official-Site Snapshot (Checked 9 November 2026): Bitget's homepage counter displays 81+ mainnets, 14K+ tokens, and 90M+ users, while other official sections describe 130+ blockchains, 1M+ assets, and 100M+ users. These are company-published figures, not independently audited live metrics—always verify current figures and release notes on official pages.

⭐ 2. Key Features

Bitget Wallet combines multi-chain asset management, swap routing, DApp discovery, and payment tools:

  • Self-Custody Architecture: Manage your own wallet credentials and take direct responsibility for offline backups and transaction approvals.
  • Multi-Chain Access: Explore supported assets and networks across major EVM and non-EVM ecosystems from a single interface.
  • Swap & Cross-Chain Routing: Swap supported tokens and use cross-chain routes where available; always compare quotes, routes, and slippage first.
  • Pay, Earn & DApp Explore: Access supported payment, staking/earning, DApp discovery, and tokenized-asset services where eligible in your region.

Understanding network, swap, and service fees helps you avoid unexpected charges during transfers or swaps:

App & Extension Download Downloading Bitget Wallet on mobile or Chrome and creating/importing a wallet is free.

Swap & Cross-Chain Fees Swaps and cross-chain bridges involve network gas, route/provider fees, and potential slippage—always inspect the quote before confirming.
Payments & Regional Services Fiat on/off-ramps, payment cards, and third-party services carry provider-specific fees and regional eligibility limits.

🗝️ 5. Custody & Control

Bitget describes Bitget Wallet as a self-custodial wallet, putting key management and signing control directly in your hands:

  • Full Credential Responsibility: You control your seed phrase, private keys, and passcodes. Store phrase backups offline and never share them with support or third parties.
  • Irreversible Transactions: If you lose wallet access without a valid backup, recovery may not be possible, and confirmed on-chain transactions cannot be reversed.

❓ 10. FAQ

1. Is Bitget Wallet self-custodial?

What it is: Yes—Bitget describes Bitget Wallet as a self-custodial wallet, meaning you control your wallet credentials rather than a centralized exchange.

How to stay safe: Back up your recovery phrase offline and review every transaction signature carefully.

2. Which networks does Bitget Wallet support?

What is supported: The official site lists major networks such as Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, BNB Chain, TRON, Polygon, Arbitrum, and Base, along with dozens of additional mainnets.

How to check: Coverage and supported actions vary by chain—always check the live network directory at web3.bitget.com/wallet/mainnets.

3. Can I swap tokens across chains?

What it is: Yes—cross-chain swap routes are available for supported assets and networks inside the wallet.

How to check: Review the quote, bridge route, slippage tolerance, gas fees, and destination network before confirming.

4. Does Bitget Wallet have a Chrome extension?

What it is: Yes—the official website links to its Chrome browser extension alongside iOS and Android mobile apps.

How to stay safe: Open web3.bitget.com/wallet first to navigate to the verified Chrome Web Store listing and avoid fake extensions.

5. Are payment cards and cash-out available everywhere?

What to know: No—card, fiat on-ramp/off-ramp, QR payment, and earning availability depend on your country, account eligibility, provider, and current product terms.

How to check: Check regional eligibility inside the official app or help center before relying on payment features.

6. Can support recover my seed phrase?

What to know: No—never share a seed phrase or private key with support or anyone else. Anyone asking for it is a major scam warning sign.

How to stay safe: If you lose wallet access and have no valid backup, recovery may not be possible, so store backups securely offline.
