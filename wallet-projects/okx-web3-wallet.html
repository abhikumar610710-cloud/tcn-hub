1 What is OKX Web3 Wallet?

OKX Web3 Wallet is a non-custodial, multi-chain wallet designed to help users manage digital assets and connect to decentralized applications (DApps). It is available through the OKX app, browser extension and Web3 website. Users can create a wallet or import an existing wallet they control.

The Web3 Wallet is distinct from an OKX centralized exchange account. With a self-custody wallet, you control the recovery phrase or private keys. OKX cannot recover a lost seed phrase or private key, so secure backup is essential.

💡

Quick Fact

OKX says its Web3 Wallet supports 80+ networks, including EVM, Bitcoin, Solana, TON, Sui and Aptos. The available networks and features can vary by app, extension and region.

6 Security Tips

  • 🛡️Protect your seed phrase. Never share it with anyone or enter it into a website sent by another person.
  • 🔎Verify the official app. Download from OKX's official website or a verified app-store listing.
  • 📋Review transaction details. Check the network, address, token, amount and estimated fees before signing.
  • ⚠️Be careful with tokens and DApps. Risk alerts are helpful, but they cannot guarantee that an asset or application is safe.
  • 🔐Keep backup passwords safe. If you use cloud backup, protect the backup password and associated account.
