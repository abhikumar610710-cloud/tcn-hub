🌐 1. Overview

OKX Web3 Wallet is a self-custody (non-custodial) multi-chain wallet designed to help users manage supported crypto assets and NFTs and connect to decentralized applications (dApps) across multiple blockchains.

Even though it is offered under the OKX ecosystem, a self-custody wallet works differently from a standard custodial exchange account: you control your wallet access/recovery credentials directly, and you must carefully verify networks, addresses, and transaction prompts before signing.

What It Helps You Do

  • Multi-Chain Asset & NFT Management: Manage supported tokens and NFTs across multiple networks, connect to dApps, and access swap or Web3 tools from mobile or browser.
  • Independent Self-Custody: Control your wallet credentials directly rather than relying on custodial exchange storage.

What to Keep in Mind

  • Backup & Verification Responsibility: Protecting recovery details, checking network compatibility, and reviewing permissions before approving transactions rests with you.

⭐ 2. Key Features

OKX Web3 Wallet brings multi-chain asset management, dApp access, and transaction checks together:

  • Multi-Chain Wallet Experience: Supports switching and viewing supported assets across multiple EVM and non-EVM networks within one wallet interface.
  • Swap & dApp Connectivity: Allows connecting to supported dApps and reviewing swap/transaction routes and quotes before confirming.
  • NFT & Collectible Management: Lets you view and manage supported NFTs and digital assets on compatible networks.
  • Transaction & Approval Checks: Displays transaction details and token/contract prompts so you can inspect permissions before signing.

💸 3. Fee

Understanding how fees apply on a self-custody Web3 wallet helps prevent failed transactions or surprises:

Wallet Setup & Holding Creating or importing a self-custody wallet is free; there is no recurring fee just for holding a wallet.
Network / Gas Fees Onchain transfers, token approvals, and contract interactions require network gas paid in the active chain's native token (such as ETH, SOL, or another chain's gas asset).
Swap, Bridge & dApp Charges Swaps, bridges, or third-party dApps may involve network gas, routing/service fees, or slippage/spread—always check the exact quote before signing.
Wrong Network Risk Sending funds on an unsupported or mismatched network can result in loss or recovery difficulty—always verify network support before sending.

🗝️ 5. Custody & Control

As a self-custody wallet, you hold control over your wallet keys or recovery credentials rather than relying on a third party to hold them for you:

  • Recovery Phrase & Backup Control: Whoever holds your recovery phrase or backup credentials can access the wallet—store backups securely and privately.
  • Irreversible Onchain Actions: Once a blockchain transaction is signed and confirmed, it cannot simply be reversed by support—verify details before approving.

🛡️ 7. Security

Follow strict security habits whenever you connect to dApps, sign messages, or transfer assets:

Use a Secondary Wallet Use a secondary wallet with limited funds when testing new dApps, airdrops, or unfamiliar links so your main wallet stays protected.
Never Share Recovery Phrase Never share your recovery phrase or private keys with anyone—including anyone claiming to be support—and never enter them on websites or chats.
Inspect Approvals & Signatures Check token approvals, spending limits, and transaction prompts carefully before signing, and revoke unused permissions when needed.
Verify URLs & Networks Double-check official domains and selected networks before sending funds or interacting with smart contracts.

❓ 10. FAQ

1. Is OKX Web3 Wallet a self-custody wallet?

What it is: Yes—OKX Web3 Wallet is a self-custody wallet where you control your wallet access and recovery credentials rather than relying on custodial exchange storage.

How to use it: Keep your recovery phrase private and offline, and verify every network and transaction prompt yourself.

2. What fees apply when using OKX Web3 Wallet?

What fees apply: Creating or importing a wallet is free, while onchain transactions require network gas fees and swaps, bridges, or dApps may involve gas, service/routing fees, or slippage/spread.

How to check: Review the exact fee, gas token, and output details shown before confirming a transaction.

3. Which networks does OKX Web3 Wallet support?

What is supported: It supports multiple EVM and non-EVM blockchains, with exact network availability subject to updates in official documentation.

How to check: Confirm that your target chain is supported and matches the receiving network before transferring funds.

4. Can I use OKX Web3 Wallet on mobile and desktop?

What it is: Yes—it is available through official mobile app and browser extension options listed on OKX's official portal.

How to set up: Always install from okx.com/web3 or verified official app/extension stores.

5. Why should I use a secondary wallet and review approvals?

What it is: A secondary wallet keeps limited funds separate for testing new dApps or links, and token approvals control contract access to your tokens.

How to stay safe: Use a secondary wallet on unfamiliar sites, inspect approval requests before signing, and revoke unused permissions.

6. Can support recover a lost recovery phrase or reverse a transfer?

What to know: With self-custody wallets, lost recovery credentials cannot simply be reset like a typical account password, and confirmed blockchain transfers generally cannot be undone.

How to stay safe: Never share your recovery phrase with anyone claiming to be support and always test or double-check details before sending.
