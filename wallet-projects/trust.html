🛡️ 1. Overview

Trust Wallet is a self-custody (non-custodial) cryptocurrency wallet available as a mobile app and browser extension. It helps users store, send, receive, swap, and manage supported digital assets across 100+ blockchains, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, Solana, and Polygon.

Because it is self-custody, private keys and recovery credentials are controlled by you rather than held on a custodial exchange. Trust Wallet cannot access your recovery phrase or move funds for you, so keeping your recovery backup safe and offline is essential.

What It Helps You Do

  • Multi-Chain Asset & NFT Management: Store, send, receive, and swap supported tokens and NFTs across 100+ blockchains, connect to dApps, and access staking or backup options.

What to Keep in Mind

  • Exact Network Matching & Recovery: Sending/receiving networks must match exactly, network gas fees and third-party service charges can apply, and lost recovery credentials cannot simply be reset.

⭐ 2. Key Features

Trust Wallet combines multi-chain asset management, dApp access, and safety checks across supported networks:

  • 100+ Blockchains & Send/Receive: Manage assets across major networks like Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, and BNB Smart Chain, and send or receive supported tokens to compatible addresses.
  • Swaps, NFTs & Staking: Swap supported assets through available routes, view and manage NFTs, and access staking/earning options for supported assets where rewards vary.
  • Security Scanner & dApp Access: Screen known risk signals with the Security Scanner and connect to dApps through supported wallet connection flows.
  • Backup & Hardware Support: Use encrypted cloud backup options where available or connect a supported Ledger hardware wallet.

💸 3. Fee

Understanding how wallet and network fees work helps you plan transfers and swaps accurately:

App Download & Wallet Creation Downloading Trust Wallet and creating or importing a wallet is free; there is no recurring subscription fee just to hold a wallet.
Network / Gas Fees Sending crypto, interacting with contracts, or executing onchain actions requires network gas paid in the active chain's native asset.
Swap, Service & Staking Terms Swaps and optional third-party services may involve network gas, provider/service fees, or slippage/spread, and staking rewards/terms can change over time.
Mismatched Network Risk Sending tokens on an unsupported or mismatched network can result in loss or recovery difficulty—always verify network support before sending.

🗝️ 5. Custody & Control

Trust Wallet is a self-custody wallet, meaning you maintain direct control over your private keys and recovery phrase rather than handing custody to an exchange:

  • Secret Recovery Phrase Responsibility: Your recovery phrase is the master backup to your self-custody wallet—store it offline in a safe, private place and never share it with anyone.
  • No Custodial Recovery by Support: Trust Wallet cannot recover your wallet without the required recovery phrase, and confirmed blockchain transactions cannot simply be reversed.

🛡️ 7. Security

Combine strong device/backup habits with transaction checks—and treat warnings as helpful signals rather than a complete guarantee:

Use a Secondary Wallet Use a secondary wallet with limited funds when testing new dApps, links, or unfamiliar contracts so your main funds stay isolated.
Never Share Recovery Phrase Never share your recovery phrase (such as a 12-word phrase) with anyone—support will never ask you to send it by message, email, or chat.
Security Scanner Limits Use the Security Scanner to spot known risk warnings, but remember a clean scan is not a guarantee that a token, site, or contract is safe.
Review Transactions & Networks Verify destination address, token, amount, network, and permissions before signing, and protect your device and offline backups.

❓ 10. FAQ

1. Is Trust Wallet free to download?

What it is: Yes—Trust Wallet is free to download and use, while blockchain gas fees and third-party service fees may apply to transactions and optional features.

How to check: Review network gas and quote details before confirming any transaction or swap.

2. How many blockchains does Trust Wallet support?

What is supported: Trust Wallet supports 100+ blockchains, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, Solana, and Polygon, with coverage subject to updates.

How to check: Confirm current chain support in the app and official Trust Wallet pages before transferring assets.

3. Can I use Trust Wallet on a computer and mobile?

What it is: Yes—Trust Wallet provides mobile apps (iOS and Android) as well as browser extensions for supported browsers including Chrome, Brave, Edge, and Opera.

How to set up: Download only from trustwallet.com/download or verified official stores and import your wallet safely.

4. Can Trust Wallet support recover my wallet or recovery phrase?

What to know: No—because Trust Wallet is self-custody, support cannot recover your wallet without the required recovery phrase.

How to stay safe: Never share your recovery phrase by message, email, or chat—anyone asking for it is a scam risk.

5. Does the Security Scanner guarantee a token or site is safe?

What to know: No—while the Security Scanner helps flag known risk signals, a clean scan does not guarantee that a token, website, or smart contract is safe.

How to stay safe: Use a secondary wallet for testing, review every transaction detail carefully, and do your own research.

6. Why must I check network compatibility before sending crypto?

What to know: Tokens with the same name can exist on different blockchains, and sending across an incompatible or wrong network can cause loss or recovery issues.

How to stay safe: Always match the sending and receiving networks exactly and test with a small transfer first.
