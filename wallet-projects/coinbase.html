🐰 1. Overview

Rabby Wallet (developed by the DeBank team) is a self-custody (non-custodial) cryptocurrency wallet built specifically for Ethereum and EVM-compatible DeFi and Web3 workflows. It focuses on giving users clear pre-signing transaction previews, automatic chain switching, and multi-chain EVM asset visibility.

Why use a wallet like Rabby? For multi-chain EVM users, it helps inspect balance changes before signing and reduces manual chain-switching mistakes—while keeping full key control in your hands.

What It Helps You Do

  • Pre-Sign Balance Previews: Inspect estimated asset and balance changes before signing a supported transaction.
  • Automatic Chain Switching: Smoothly switches to the EVM network required by a connected dApp.
  • Approval & Portfolio Management: Review token approvals and view multi-chain EVM balances in one place.

What to Keep in Mind

  • Estimates vs. Guarantees: Simulation previews are helpful aids rather than a guarantee against all smart contract or phishing risks.
  • Self-Custody Responsibility: Full backup and key protection responsibility stays with you.
Key Fact: Rabby is a non-custodial wallet interface for Ethereum and EVM networks—your assets stay on the blockchain ledger while your keys remain under your control.

⭐ 2. Key Features

Rabby Wallet provides practical tools tailored for multi-chain EVM users and DeFi activity:

  • Transaction Simulation & Previews: Shows estimated asset and balance changes before you sign a supported transaction so you can verify what is leaving and arriving.
  • Automatic Network Switching: Detects the EVM network required by a connected dApp and helps switch smoothly instead of manual network toggling errors.
  • Risk Alerts & Approval Manager: Flags potential contract or approval risks before confirmation and lets you inspect or revoke token allowances/approvals.
  • Hardware Wallet & Portfolio View: Supports connecting hardware signers and viewing multi-chain EVM balances, collectibles, and swaps within one interface.

💸 3. Fee

Understanding fee components on Rabby Wallet helps you plan transactions and avoid unexpected costs:

Wallet Setup & Viewing Installing Rabby and viewing wallet addresses or balances is free. Onchain actions carry network gas and applicable service charges.
Network / Gas Fees Every onchain transfer, swap, or contract approval requires gas paid in the active EVM network's native gas token (such as ETH, POL, BNB, or AVAX).
Rabby Built-In Swap Fee Rabby's built-in swap charges a 0.25% Rabby swap fee (subject to specific pair/stablecoin terms where applicable), alongside underlying gas fees, route/liquidity costs, and slippage.
Third-Party dApps & Bridges External dApps, bridges, or liquidity protocols may apply their own protocol fees or price impact—always check the full summary before signing.

🗝️ 5. Custody & Control

Rabby Wallet operates as a self-custody wallet where full control and backup responsibility stay with you:

  • Secret Recovery Phrase & Keys: Your Secret Recovery Phrase or private keys control access to your funds. Anyone who obtains them can transfer your assets, and losing them without a backup is permanent.
  • No Custodial Recovery by Support: Wallet support cannot access your keys, reset a lost Secret Recovery Phrase, or reverse confirmed blockchain transactions.

🛠️ 6. How to Use

Follow these steps when setting up and using Rabby Wallet:

1

Step 1: Visit the Official Website Only

Go directly to rabby.io or official verified app stores—avoid sponsored search ads or unverified links.

2

Step 2: Create, Import, or Connect a Hardware Wallet

Set a strong wallet password, create a new wallet, import an existing wallet, or connect a supported hardware wallet.

3

Step 3: Back Up Your Secret Recovery Phrase Offline

Write your recovery words on paper in exact order and keep them offline in a secure place. Never take screenshots or store them in cloud notes or chats.

4

Step 4: Inspect Transaction Simulation & Send a Test Amount

Review the simulation preview, network, recipient address, and gas cost, and test with a small amount first before larger transfers.

5

Pause on any risk warning, review token approvals carefully, and revoke unneeded permissions or disconnect unused dApps.

🛡️ 7. Security

Even with helpful simulation and risk alerts, safe habits are essential in DeFi and Web3:

Never Share Recovery Phrase or Keys Never share your Secret Recovery Phrase, private keys, or password with anyone—including anyone claiming to be Rabby/DeBank support or community moderators.
Use a Secondary Wallet Use a secondary wallet with limited funds when exploring new dApps, mints, or unfamiliar links, keeping main funds separate.
Treat Simulations as Aid, Not a Guarantee Review transaction previews, token approvals, and destination addresses yourself; automated simulations can miss novel drainers or phishing sites.
Verify URLs & Keep Native Gas Ready Always check exact domains, avoid phishing popups, and ensure you have enough native gas token on the target EVM network.

❓ 10. FAQ

1. What is Rabby Wallet and how do you use it safely?

What it is: Rabby Wallet is a self-custody wallet built for Ethereum and EVM-compatible networks with transaction simulation, automatic network switching, and risk alerts.

How to use it: Install only from rabby.io or official app stores, back up your Secret Recovery Phrase offline, verify simulation previews and addresses, and test with a small transfer first.

2. Which networks does Rabby Wallet support?

What it supports: Rabby supports Ethereum and a broad range of EVM-compatible networks (140+ EVM chains) plus custom EVM networks, while focusing on the EVM ecosystem rather than native non-EVM chains like Bitcoin or Solana.

How to use it: Confirm the network matches your asset and dApp, and check official documentation when adding custom networks or bridging assets.

3. What fees apply on Rabby Wallet (gas, 0.25% swap fee, and network costs)?

What fees apply: Basic wallet use is free, while onchain transactions require blockchain gas fees and Rabby's built-in swap charges a 0.25% Rabby swap fee (subject to applicable pair terms) alongside gas, route/liquidity costs, and slippage.

How to check: Always inspect the estimated gas, fee breakdown, and output amount on screen before approving a transaction or swap.

4. How does Transaction Simulation work and why isn't it a 100% safety guarantee?

What it is: Transaction simulation previews expected balance changes and flags potential risks before you sign, helping you spot unexpected outflows or risky approvals.

How to use it: Read the preview carefully before signing, but never rely on it alone—always verify the website URL, contract, and approval scope yourself.

5. Why should I use a secondary wallet with Rabby?

What it is: A secondary wallet holds a small balance for testing new dApps, claims, or unfamiliar links while keeping your main holdings separate.

How to use it: Connect your secondary wallet first on new or experimental sites so a malicious approval or phishing link cannot drain your primary funds.

6. How do token approvals work and how should I manage them?

What it is: Token approvals let a smart contract spend a specified amount of a token on your behalf—unlimited or high approvals stay active until changed or revoked.

How to manage them: Check approval amounts during signing, avoid unnecessary unlimited approvals on untrusted contracts, and revoke old or unused approvals when no longer needed.

7. Can Rabby support recover my Secret Recovery Phrase or reverse a transaction?

What to know: Because Rabby is self-custody and blockchain transactions are final once confirmed, support cannot recover a lost Secret Recovery Phrase or undo an onchain transfer.

How to stay safe: Keep your recovery phrase offline, never share it with anyone claiming to be support, and use official channels from rabby.io.

8. Can I use Rabby across browser, mobile, and hardware wallets?

What is available: Rabby is available across browser extensions, desktop/mobile apps, and hardware wallet connections such as Ledger, Trezor, Keystone, and OneKey.

How to use it: Download only from official sources on each device, keep your software updated, and protect your recovery phrase offline.
