1 What is Ledger Nano?

Ledger Nano is a family of hardware devices designed to secure the private keys used to access crypto assets. The crypto itself remains recorded on its blockchain; the device protects keys and lets you review and approve supported transactions on the device.

Ledger's companion application is now called Ledger Wallet (formerly Ledger Live). It helps users manage accounts, install blockchain apps on the device and access supported services. Availability and features can vary by device, asset, region and third-party provider.

💡

Quick Fact

A hardware signer is not an exchange account. You remain responsible for your PIN, recovery phrase and transaction approvals. Never type your recovery phrase into a website, chat, phone or computer.

6 Security Tips

  • 🛡️Never share your recovery phrase. Ledger support will never need you to reveal your 24 words. Anyone with the phrase can restore your wallet.
  • 🔎Download the app from Ledger. Avoid search-ad lookalikes, unofficial installers and links sent by strangers.
  • ✋Verify details on the device. Check the recipient address, network and transaction information before approving.
  • 📦Check device authenticity. Complete Ledger's Genuine Check in Ledger Wallet. Buy from Ledger or an authorized reseller.
  • 🔒Keep the backup offline. Do not photograph, email, cloud-store or paste your recovery phrase into any app or site.
