1 What is Bitget Wallet?

Bitget Wallet is a non-custodial, multi-chain cryptocurrency wallet. It provides tools to manage supported digital assets, connect to decentralized applications (DApps), swap tokens and move assets across supported networks. The official site lists mobile apps and a Chrome extension.

Self-custody means you are responsible for protecting your wallet access and approving transactions. Your assets are recorded on their respective blockchains; the wallet is the interface used to manage access. Never assume that a token, DApp, bridge or transaction is safe just because it appears inside a wallet.

5 Security Tips

  • 🛡️Keep recovery details private. Never give your seed phrase, private key, passcode or verification code to anyone.
  • 🔎Verify downloads and links. Start at the official Bitget Wallet website; do not trust ads, unsolicited messages or lookalike domains.
  • ✋Review every signature. Check token, network, recipient, permissions, fees and approval details before confirming.
  • 🔐Use risk checks, not blind trust. Security alerts and audits can reduce risk but cannot guarantee that every token, DApp or contract is safe.
  • 🧾Recheck token approvals. Periodically review and revoke permissions you no longer need using trusted tools.

Bitget describes encryption, transaction-risk screening, phishing detection and third-party security reviews on its security page. These are company disclosures, not a guarantee against loss. Read the official security details ↗
