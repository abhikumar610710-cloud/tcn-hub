❓ 10. FAQ

1. What is CoinSwitch?

What it is: An India-focused crypto trading and investing platform whose current website lists Spot, Futures, Options, Web3 coins and related tools.

How to check: Confirm current product availability and terms on the official platform.

2. Are the prices on this page CoinSwitch quotes?

What they are: Indicative INR reference prices from CoinGecko—not CoinSwitch order-book quotes or executable prices.

How to check: Actual CoinSwitch prices, spreads and fees can differ on the trading screen.

3. Does CoinSwitch support INR trading?

What to know: CoinSwitch advertises INR-based Spot and derivatives products.

How to check: Supported pairs, settlement methods, deposits and withdrawals can change—verify inside the official platform.

4. Is CoinSwitch FIU registered?

What to know: CoinSwitch's AML policy states that Bitcipher Labs LLP, operating under the CoinSwitch brand, is registered with FIU-IND as a reporting entity under the PMLA.

How to keep in mind: Registration does not make crypto assets risk-free or guarantee returns.

5. Can I use the CoinSwitch API on this page?

What to know: No—this page does not connect accounts or place trades.

How to do it safely: Implement CoinSwitch PRO APIs securely on a server using official docs and never expose secrets in browser code.

6. Are Futures and Options risk-free?

What to know: No—derivatives can magnify losses and leveraged positions may be liquidated.

How to check: Review contract, margin, fees, funding and expiry terms before trading.
