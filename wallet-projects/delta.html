🏛️ 1. Overview

Delta Exchange India is a digital-asset trading platform operated by Excelium Technologies Private Limited. Its India platform offers Spot trading in BTC, ETH, SOL and XRP, along with crypto derivatives such as Futures and Options on Bitcoin and Ether, plus selected real-world-asset products and trading tools.

Spot trading means buying or selling a supported asset directly without derivatives leverage, whereas Futures and Options involve contract terms, margin, expiry or funding mechanics and can create rapid losses.

⭐ 2. Key Features

Delta Exchange India combines non-leveraged Spot markets with derivatives and automation tools:

  • Spot Trading: Buy and sell currently supported BTC, ETH, SOL and XRP spot markets without derivatives leverage.
  • Futures & Perpetuals: Explore crypto derivative contracts; review contract specifications, margin, funding and liquidation rules carefully.
  • Options: BTC and ETH call and put options are described in Delta's India product guides.
  • Trading Tools & Quotation vs. Settlement: Includes APIs, TradingView automation, strategy builder, analytics and demo trading. For example, BTCUSD contract price may be quoted in USD per BTC while margin/effective settlement is INR—always check contract specs for quote currency, lot size and settlement.

❓ 10. FAQ

1. What is Delta Exchange India?

What it is: A digital-asset trading platform operated by Excelium Technologies Private Limited, offering Spot markets, crypto derivatives (Futures & Options) and other listed trading products.

How to check: Verify current product availability on the official site.

2. Which coins are available in Spot trading?

What is listed: Delta Exchange's Spot help article lists Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL) and Ripple (XRP).

How to check: Confirm supported pairs and availability on the live platform.

3. Are the market prices on this page exact order quotes?

What they are: Values returned by Delta's public Spot ticker API at the most recent refresh—not guaranteed execution prices.

How to check: Order book, spread and available quantity can change before an order is placed.

4. Does Delta Exchange India have FIU registration?

What is reported: Delta's official Help Center states that Excelium Technologies Private Limited is an FIU-IND registered reporting entity (VA00041101).

How to keep in mind: Registration is not a guarantee against trading or custody risks.

5. Do public API prices need an API key?

What to know: The documented public market ticker endpoint does not require an API key, whereas account and order endpoints require authentication.

How to stay safe: Never publish private API credentials in website source code.

6. Are Futures and Options risk-free?

What to know: No—derivatives involve contract-specific margin, expiry or funding rules, and leverage can magnify losses or lead to liquidation.

How to check: Read contract specifications carefully before trading.
