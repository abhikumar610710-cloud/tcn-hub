1 What is Tangem Wallet?

Tangem Wallet is a non-custodial hardware wallet. Its physical device is a card or ring containing a secure element chip. The device has no battery, screen or internet connection; it communicates with a compatible smartphone through NFC. The Tangem app acts as the interface for viewing balances, managing supported assets and initiating transactions.

Tangem's official site describes support for 14,100+ assets across 90+ blockchain networks. Network and token availability can change, and a supported token may have different features depending on its network.

💡

Important distinction

Tangem is a wallet, not a centralized crypto exchange. Buy, swap and earn options inside the app may be provided by third parties. Rates, availability, fees and regional access can vary.

3 How to Set Up Tangem Wallet

These are general steps for a second-generation Tangem hardware wallet. Exact screens can vary by app version and device generation. Follow the instructions shown in the official app.

1

Get the Official App

Use Tangem's official website to reach the App Store or Google Play listing. Check the publisher before installing.

2

Scan Your Device

Enable NFC and tap your Tangem card or ring against the phone when prompted.

3

Create a Wallet

Choose the wallet setup method. Seedless setup is available; seed phrase options depend on device generation.

4

Set Up Backups

Link one or two additional devices from the same compatible set and create an access code.

5

Add Assets Carefully

Add supported coins and tokens, verify each network and test with a small transfer first.

Backup reminder: For second-generation hardware wallets, Tangem's seedless setup requires at least two devices. Keep backup devices safe and separate. If you choose a seed phrase setup, store the phrase offline and never photograph, screenshot or share it.

5 Security Tips

  • 🛡️Buy from a trusted source. Prefer Tangem's official store or an authorized seller. During setup, follow the app's authenticity checks.
  • 🔑Protect your access code. Never share it. Keep backup cards or rings in separate secure places.
  • 🧾Protect any seed phrase. A seed phrase is optional on supported generations. If you use one, keep it offline and private.
  • 🔎Verify every transaction. Check the recipient address, blockchain network, token contract and amount before signing.
  • 🚫Ignore support impostors. No legitimate support agent needs your private key, seed phrase or access code.
