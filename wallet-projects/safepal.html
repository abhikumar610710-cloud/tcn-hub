🏛️ 1. Overview

SafePal S1 is a hardware wallet designed to keep the private keys used to access supported crypto assets offline. It uses QR-code scanning between the S1 device and the SafePal App for normal wallet pairing and transaction signing—the app communicates with blockchains while private keys stay on the offline hardware device.

SafePal describes S1 as supporting 200+ blockchains, though exact coins, tokens, networks and features can change with firmware and app versions.

Self-Custody & Offline Key Rule: The S1 is not an exchange account and does not store coins inside the device. Assets remain on their blockchains; keep your mnemonic phrase private and offline, and never share it with support agents or enter it on a website.

🗝️ 5. Custody & Mnemonic Backup

A wallet can generally be restored on a compatible wallet using the correct mnemonic phrase and any passphrase you configured—while SafePal states it does not store your mnemonic phrase and cannot retrieve it for you.

Mnemonic Phrase Rule: Write your own recovery phrase offline during setup and verify it; if lost or exposed, recovery may not be possible, and any request to reveal or type it into a site, app or chat is a serious scam warning.

❓ 10. FAQ

1. Does SafePal S1 use Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, and how does signing work?

Air-gapped QR flow: S1 lists no Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or NFC—normal pairing and transaction signing use QR codes with the SafePal App while private keys stay offline on the device (firmware updates separately use a PC and USB cable in upgrade mode).

2. How do I check whether my S1 is genuine?

Device authentication: Complete the official authentication check when starting the wallet for the first time, and stop using the device if it reports an authenticity issue or prior use.

3. What happens if I lose S1, and can SafePal recover my mnemonic phrase?

Recovery & safety: You can generally restore on a compatible wallet using your mnemonic phrase and any passphrase (if lost or exposed, recovery may not be possible), and SafePal does not store or retrieve your phrase—never share or type it into a site, app or chat.

4. Should I back up before a firmware update?

Pre-update backup: Yes—ensure your mnemonic phrase is securely backed up before starting a firmware upgrade and download firmware only from SafePal's official site.
