1 What is SafePal S1?

SafePal S1 is a hardware wallet designed to keep the private keys used to access supported crypto assets offline. It uses QR-code scanning between the S1 device and the SafePal App for normal wallet pairing and transaction signing. The app can communicate with blockchains, while the private keys stay on the offline hardware device.

SafePal describes S1 as supporting 200+ blockchains. The exact coins, tokens, networks and features available can change with firmware and app versions, so check the official asset list before receiving or sending an asset.

💡

Quick Fact

The S1 is not an exchange account and does not store coins inside the device. Your assets remain on their blockchains; the hardware wallet protects the keys used to authorize transactions. Keep your mnemonic phrase private and offline. Never share it with support agents or enter it on a website.

6 Security Tips

  • 🛡️Never share your mnemonic phrase. Anyone with the phrase may be able to control your assets. SafePal support should never ask you to disclose it.
  • 🔎Authenticate the device. Complete the official S1 authentication flow when first starting a new device. Stop if it reports that the device is not genuine or was previously used.
  • ✋Verify transaction details. Check the destination address, network and amount on the hardware device before approving.
  • 🔒Set a strong PIN. Avoid simple combinations and keep the device physically secure.
  • 💾Back up before firmware updates. SafePal specifically advises securing the mnemonic phrase before beginning an S1 firmware upgrade.
