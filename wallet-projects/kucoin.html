1 What is KuCoin?

KuCoin is a centralized cryptocurrency exchange (CEX) that offers services such as Spot trading, Margin trading, Futures, Convert, KuCoin Earn, Trading Bot, ETFs, KuCoin Pay and other products. Its exchange account is a custodial platform account, which is different from holding assets in a self-custody wallet.

The company's About Us page currently states 45 million global users and more than 1,000 listed tokens. These are figures published by KuCoin and may change; check the official page for the latest company profile. Review the terms, supported assets, networks, fees and risks before depositing or trading.

5 Account Security Tips

  • 🔐Enable Google 2FA or a passkey. Secure your email and device as well as your exchange account.
  • 📩Set an anti-phishing safety phrase. Check it in genuine KuCoin messages and login pages; stop if it is missing or incorrect.
  • 🌐Use the Official Verification Center. Verify a website, email or social account before trusting it.
  • 📍Review login and withdrawal protections. Use available IP restrictions and withdrawal safeguards where appropriate.
  • ✋Never share secrets or codes. Do not disclose passwords, OTPs, 2FA codes, API secrets or remote-access permissions to anyone.

See KuCoin's current security guidance for account protection settings and scam warnings. Security features cannot remove every risk. KuCoin Security Notice ↗ · Google 2FA guide ↗ · Passkeys guide ↗
