🏛️ 1. Overview

KuCoin is a centralized cryptocurrency exchange (CEX) that offers Spot trading, Margin trading, Futures, Convert, KuCoin Earn, Trading Bot, ETFs, KuCoin Pay, and related products. Its exchange account is a custodial platform account, which differs from holding assets in a self-custody wallet.

KuCoin's About Us page reports 45M global users, 1,000+ listed tokens, and presence across 200+ countries & regions (company-published figures that may change). Verify details at kucoin.com/about-us.

✅ Potential Advantages

Multiple trading and conversion products, public Spot market-data API, KCS ecosystem fee features, and published Proof of Reserves reports.

⚠️ Things to Consider

Exchange custody and platform risk, variable fees by pair/tier, geographic restrictions, and magnified liquidation risk on Margin and Futures.

🌏 Important for Users in India & Other Regions: KuCoin's terms state that access to platform services may be restricted or prohibited based on geographic location. This guide does not confirm that every KuCoin service is available in India. Check the current terms, account-specific notices, and applicable Indian laws and tax requirements before registering, depositing, or trading. KuCoin Terms of Use ↗

❓ 10. FAQ

1. Is KuCoin a wallet or an exchange?

What it is: This page covers KuCoin Exchange, a centralized exchange (CEX). Exchange accounts and self-custody wallets have different custody and access arrangements.

How to stay safe: Confirm which product you are using before depositing or storing assets.

2. Does KuCoin require identity verification?

What to know: KuCoin states that new users must complete Identity Verification (KYC) to use its products and services, while older accounts may have different restrictions.

How to check: Review the current official KYC guide and your account verification status.

3. Are BTC, ETH, and KCS prices on this page live?

What it is: The market cards request KuCoin public Spot ticker snapshots and refresh about every 30 seconds.

How to check: They are indicative quotes in USDT rather than a guaranteed tick-by-tick feed; check KuCoin Markets for the live trading screen.

4. How much are KuCoin trading fees?

What to know: Fees depend on trading pair, maker/taker role, VIP fee level, and applicable discounts such as KCS fee payment.

How to check: Review the exact fee displayed on the official trading page before placing an order.

5. Does Proof of Reserves remove all risk?

What to know: No—Proof of Reserves is a transparency measure and does not remove exchange, custody, operational, market, or regulatory risks.

How to check: Read the full report, snapshot date, and audit methodology on KuCoin's official Proof of Reserves page.

6. Can everyone use KuCoin in every country?

What to know: No—KuCoin's terms allow geographic restrictions and service limitations depending on local regulations.

How to check: Check the current Terms of Use and your account-specific eligibility for your location before using the platform.
