1 What is Trezor Safe?

Trezor Safe is a family of hardware wallets that protects the private keys used to access supported crypto assets. Your coins and tokens remain recorded on their respective blockchains; the hardware wallet keeps signing keys under your control and lets you verify and approve supported transactions on the device.

Trezor Suite is Trezor's companion app for managing accounts, checking balances and transaction history, and accessing supported send, receive, buy, sell, swap and staking features. Availability varies by asset, device, app version, provider and region.

💡

Quick Fact

A Trezor is not an exchange account or a place where blockchain assets physically sit. You are responsible for your device PIN, wallet backup and transaction approvals. Never type your wallet backup into a website, chat, phone or computer.

6 Security Tips

  • 🛡️Never share your wallet backup. No legitimate Trezor support agent should ask you to reveal the backup words.
  • 🔎Download Trezor Suite only from Trezor. Use the official download page and verify the desktop app where applicable.
  • ✋Verify on the device screen. Check addresses, amounts, networks and transaction details before confirming.
  • 📦Check the package and device. Inspect tamper-evident packaging and follow Trezor's device-safety checks.
  • 🔒Keep the backup offline. Do not photograph, email, cloud-store or paste backup words into any app or website.
