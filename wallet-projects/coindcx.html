1 What is CoinDCX?

CoinDCX is a centralized cryptocurrency platform serving users in India. Its official website lists crypto buy/sell, Spot trading, Margin, Futures, Options, Earn, trading signals and Web3 access powered by Okto. The exchange account is a custodial platform account; that is different from using a self-custody wallet where you control the recovery keys.

CoinDCX's homepage describes the platform as FIU-registered and displays ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and ISO/IEC 27701:2019 certification badges. These are company-published statements; follow the current official terms, product disclosures and local requirements before opening or using an account.

Proof of Reserves — understand what it means

CoinDCX's Support Centre describes its Proof of Reserves as a third-party verified report showing 100%+ of user assets in custody. Read the full report, snapshot date, asset scope and methodology. Proof of Reserves is a transparency measure; it is not deposit insurance and does not remove exchange, operational, custody, market or regulatory risk.

5 Security Tips

  • 🛡️Enable multi-factor authentication. Use the security options available in the official account settings.
  • 🔎Verify the website and app. Use coindcx.com and app-store links reached from the official website; watch for lookalike domains.
  • ✋Never share OTPs or passwords. CoinDCX's security page warns about impersonators; never send money or personal information to unverified contacts.
  • 🔐Protect your device and email. Keep devices updated, use a screen lock and secure the email account linked to your exchange.
  • 🧾Review every transfer and order. Check the asset, network, destination address, fees and order details before confirming.
