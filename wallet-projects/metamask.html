🦊 1. Overview — What is MetaMask?

MetaMask is a widely used self-custody (non-custodial) cryptocurrency wallet designed for managing digital assets and connecting to decentralized applications (DApps) across Ethereum and EVM-compatible blockchains. Available both as a browser extension and a mobile app, it lets you hold tokens, view NFTs, swap assets, and sign Web3 transactions.

Because MetaMask is non-custodial, you generate and hold your own 12-word Secret Recovery Phrase. Neither MetaMask nor any third party stores your funds—your assets live directly on the public blockchain, while MetaMask acts as your key manager and Web3 interface.

Why Users Choose MetaMask

  • Direct Self-Custody: You maintain ownership of your private keys and wallet accounts rather than relying on a centralized exchange.
  • Broad DApp Compatibility: Connect smoothly to DeFi platforms, NFT marketplaces, and Web3 apps across Ethereum and EVM networks.
  • Cross-Device Access: Use MetaMask across supported desktop browsers as well as Android and iOS mobile devices.
  • Flexible Network & Hardware Support: Switch between built-in networks, add custom EVM RPCs, and pair hardware wallets like Ledger or Trezor.

Practical Limitations to Keep in Mind

  • Backup Responsibility: Losing your 12-word Secret Recovery Phrase means losing access to wallet recovery—no support team can reset it.
  • Phishing & Impersonation Risks: Fake extensions and lookalike websites frequently target wallet users.
  • Irreversible On-Chain Transfers: Once a blockchain transaction confirms on-chain, it cannot simply be reversed.
Key Fact: MetaMask is a non-custodial wallet interface, not a bank account. Your coins and tokens live on the blockchain ledger, while your device holds the cryptographic keys needed to authorize transactions.

⭐ 2. Key Features

MetaMask brings together account management, network switching, token swaps, and Web3 security checks inside one workspace:

  • Self-Custody Key Vault: Keys are encrypted locally with your wallet password, allowing you to manage multiple accounts or import private keys.
  • Multi-Network & Custom RPC: Switch across Ethereum and EVM networks or add custom RPC endpoints with Network Name, RPC URL, and Chain ID.
  • Built-In Swaps & Cross-Chain Bridge: Compare swap routes or bridge supported assets directly within the wallet interface with transparent quotes.
  • DApp Connectivity & Snaps: Connect to Web3 applications, inspect transaction details before signing, and extend functionality through MetaMask Snaps.
  • Portfolio & Hardware Pairing: Track balances across accounts and connect hardware wallets such as Ledger, Trezor, Keystone, or Lattice1 for extra signing security.

💸 3. Fee & Gas Structure

Downloading and creating a MetaMask wallet is free, while on-chain transactions and optional swap/on-ramp features involve network or service costs:

Wallet Setup & Receiving Free (0 Fee) — Installing MetaMask, creating wallet accounts, and receiving supported transfers carry no wallet fee.
Network Gas Fees Every on-chain transfer, approval, or contract interaction requires gas paid in that chain's native coin (such as ETH, POL, BNB, or AVAX). Always keep native gas available before sending tokens.
Swaps & Bridge Quotes Built-in swaps and bridges display quotes, estimated gas, and any applicable service/provider fees before you confirm.
Buy / Sell On-Ramps Third-party payment or fiat on-ramp providers show their own processing rates and spreads during checkout.

🗝️ 5. Custody & Control Model

Because MetaMask is non-custodial, control stays directly with your wallet credentials:

  • 12-Word Secret Recovery Phrase: The master backup that restores all accounts derived from your wallet. Anyone holding these words can access those accounts.
  • Local Wallet Password: Unlocks MetaMask on your current device; if forgotten, you can reset it using your 12-word Secret Recovery Phrase.
  • Individual Account Private Keys: Each account address has cryptographic keys used to sign blockchain transactions.
Important Custody Rule: Never store your Secret Recovery Phrase in cloud notes, screenshots, email drafts, or chat apps, and never share it with anyone claiming to offer help.

🛠️ 6. How to Set Up & Use MetaMask

Follow these steps from official sources to set up your wallet and begin transacting safely:

1

Open the Official Website

Type https://metamask.io directly into your browser rather than clicking unverified ads or links.

2

Install the Official Extension or Mobile App

Choose your browser or mobile store from the official download page and verify the publisher before installing.

3

Create a Wallet & Set a Strong Password

Select Create a new wallet and choose a strong, unique password for local device access.

4

Record Your 12-Word Secret Recovery Phrase Offline

Write down all 12 words in exact numbered order on paper (or metal backup) and keep them in a safe physical place.

5

Verify Network, Gas & Test with a Small Amount

Check the active network and native gas token balance, verify the recipient address, and test with a small transfer first.

🛡️ 7. Security Best Practices

Consistent verification habits protect your wallet against phishing, malicious approvals, and accidental network mistakes:

Never Share Recovery Phrase No legitimate support team or DApp will ever ask for your 12-word Secret Recovery Phrase or private key.
Bookmark Official URLs Access metamask.io and trusted DApps via saved bookmarks and verify spelling before connecting.
Inspect Signatures & Approvals Review transaction prompts carefully and avoid granting unnecessary or unlimited token approvals to unfamiliar contracts.
Separate Main & Testing Wallets Keep long-term holdings separate from a secondary/burner wallet used for experimenting with new DApps or links.
Lock When Idle & Update Keep your browser, mobile OS, and wallet updated, and lock your wallet when stepping away from your device.

❓ 10. Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is MetaMask free to download and use?

Yes: Downloading MetaMask, creating accounts, and receiving supported crypto are free.

When fees apply: Blockchain gas fees apply to on-chain transactions, and optional swap/bridge/on-ramp features may carry service or provider charges.

2. What is the difference between a Public Address, Private Key, and Secret Recovery Phrase?

Public Address (0x...): Safe to share to receive funds.

Private Key: Controls a specific account; never share it.

Secret Recovery Phrase (12 words): Master backup for restoring all derived accounts—anyone with it controls those accounts.

3. Does MetaMask hold my crypto on its servers?

No: Assets stay on the blockchain ledger; MetaMask manages your encrypted keys locally on your device so you can sign transactions.

4. Can I use the same MetaMask wallet on both PC and mobile?

Yes: Choose Import an existing wallet on your second device and enter your 12-word Secret Recovery Phrase from an official download.

5. Why are my tokens not showing up after a transfer?

Check that you are viewing the exact blockchain network used for the transfer, or import the official token contract address if it is not listed automatically.

6. What if I lose my 12-word Secret Recovery Phrase?

Because MetaMask is non-custodial and does not store your recovery phrase, neither MetaMask nor anyone else can recover a wallet without your backup credentials.

7. How do I manage or revoke old DApp permissions?

Review connected sites in MetaMask and use reputable token-approval tools to check or revoke unused token allowances.
