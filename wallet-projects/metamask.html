What is MetaMask? — Understand Self-Custody

MetaMask is a self-custodial crypto wallet interface. It helps you manage blockchain accounts, view supported assets, prepare transactions and connect to decentralized applications. It is available as a browser extension and as a mobile app for supported devices.

Your assets are recorded on their respective blockchains. MetaMask provides an interface for viewing accounts and signing blockchain actions with your wallet keys. It is not a bank account, and it cannot reverse a transaction once it has been confirmed on a blockchain.

Self-custody means you control the Secret Recovery Phrase (SRP) and private keys that give access to your accounts. The SRP is the master backup for the wallet and its derived accounts. An individual private key controls a particular account.

Read this before adding funds

If you lose your SRP and also lose access to the wallet on your device, MetaMask Support cannot retrieve the phrase for you. Anyone who gets your SRP or private key can potentially control the associated accounts and transfer assets.

How to add a supported network

1

Open the network selector

In the extension, open the network dropdown from the Tokens view. In mobile, open the network dropdown at the top of the Tokens screen.

2

Choose Manage Networks

Check the additional or popular networks list and search for the chain by name or chain ID.

3

Add the network

Select the network and review its details before adding it. Prefer networks already listed by MetaMask.

Be careful with custom RPC settings

Do not copy RPC URLs or chain details from random comments, messages or unknown websites. Verify network details from the network’s own official documentation.

A token symbol can exist on multiple networks. Before sending or receiving, confirm the exact network, token contract, destination address and supported route. Two tokens with the same name are not automatically interchangeable.

Complete Setup Guide — Browser Extension and Mobile

Desktop browser setup

1

Open the official download page

Type metamask.com/download yourself or use the Get MetaMask button above. Check the domain carefully.

2

Choose your browser

Follow the official link to the browser extension store. Avoid third-party download mirrors or files sent by strangers.

3

Create a new wallet or restore one

Follow the setup prompts. If restoring, enter the SRP only inside the genuine MetaMask app or extension you installed yourself.

4

Back up your SRP offline

Write all words in the correct order and store the backup privately. Avoid screenshots, email, chat or shared cloud files.

5

Set strong device protection

Use a unique password, screen lock and current operating-system updates. Secure your email account as well.

Mobile setup

1

Use the official app-store link

Start at metamask.com/download and follow its link to the iOS App Store or Google Play listing.

2

Create or import your wallet

Follow the in-app instructions. Never enter your SRP into a website or a message from someone claiming to be support.

3

Secure your phone

Use a strong passcode and available biometric protection. Keep both the app and operating system updated.

Beginner tip

Before moving a large amount, learn how to copy an address, check a network and review a transaction. Consider a small test transfer first, while accounting for the extra network fee.

Security Center — Protect Your Secret Recovery Phrase

The Secret Recovery Phrase (SRP) is the master backup from which the wallet’s accounts are derived. Anyone with the SRP can generally restore the wallet and control the associated accounts. MetaMask Support says it will never ask for your SRP or private keys.

✍️

Write it down accurately

Record every word in the correct order and keep the backup in a private, secure place.

🚫

Never share it

Do not provide it to a person, website, support form, chat, email or remote-help session.

☁️

Avoid online copies

Avoid screenshots, cloud notes and email drafts that could be exposed if an account or device is compromised.

🔍

Check every transaction

Read the network, recipient, token, amount and approval details. Reject actions you do not understand.

🧹

Review token approvals

Some dApps request permission to spend tokens. Review allowances and revoke permissions you no longer need.

🧊

Consider a hardware wallet

A compatible hardware wallet keeps signing keys on a separate device and requires physical confirmation.

MetaMask will not ask for your recovery phrase

If someone claims to be MetaMask Support and asks for your SRP, private key or wallet password, treat it as a scam. MetaMask also cannot retrieve a lost SRP for you.

Connect to dApps — Understand Permissions and Approvals

A decentralized application (dApp) may ask to connect to MetaMask. A basic connection can let the dApp see the selected public wallet address and request actions. Connecting is different from signing a message, approving a token allowance or sending a transaction.

1

Verify the dApp website

Use the genuine project URL. Beware of look-alike domains, search ads and links in unsolicited messages.

2

Review the connection request

Check which account and network the dApp wants to access. Connect only the account you intend to use.

3

Read transaction and signature prompts

Do not approve unfamiliar messages, contract interactions or token-spending permissions.

4

Manage connected sites

Use MetaMask Permissions to review connected dApps and remove access that you no longer want.

5

Revoke token approvals separately

Disconnecting a dApp does not automatically revoke token allowances already granted on-chain.

Disconnect is not revoke

Disconnecting removes the dApp connection. Revoking removes a previously granted token-spending allowance. These are different actions, and a revoke transaction may require a network fee.

Gas Fees, Swaps, Bridges and Transaction Costs

Many blockchain actions require a network fee (gas) to be processed. This fee is paid to the network, not simply to MetaMask. It changes with network activity and transaction complexity. Different blockchains have different fee models.

⛽

Network fee / Gas

Required for many on-chain actions such as sending tokens, swaps, approvals and smart-contract interactions.

🔄

MetaMask service fee

MetaMask’s published standard fee for covered swap products is 0.875%, subject to eligible promotions and programme conditions.

🌉

Bridge provider fees

Cross-chain routes may include provider costs and network fees on the source and destination chains.

📉

Slippage and price impact

Liquidity and price movement can change the final output. Review the quote and minimum received before confirming.

Hardware Wallets — An Extra Security Layer

MetaMask documents integrations with hardware wallets such as Ledger, Trezor, Lattice and selected air-gapped devices. Compatibility and connection steps can differ between browser extension and mobile app.

🔐

Separate key protection

When properly configured, the hardware wallet signs transactions without exposing its private key to the browser.

👆

Physical confirmation

Review the destination and transaction details on the hardware device before approving.

🔗

dApp access

You can use MetaMask as an interface to connect supported hardware-wallet accounts to compatible dApps.

⚠️

Still use caution

A hardware wallet cannot prevent every scam. You can still approve a malicious transaction or reveal a recovery phrase.

Advantages, Limitations and Who It May Suit

✅

Potential advantages

Self-custody, browser and mobile access, dApp connectivity, support for multiple networks, token and NFT management, swaps and hardware-wallet integrations.

⚠️

Limitations and risks

You are responsible for recovery credentials; phishing and malicious dApps remain risks; network fees vary; and confirmed transactions are generally irreversible.

Who may find MetaMask useful?

MetaMask may suit users who want to manage supported crypto assets and interact with Web3 applications. Beginners should first learn wallet backups, network selection, transaction fees, token approvals and address verification. Start with low-risk practice before moving significant funds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MetaMask a bank or centralized exchange?

No. MetaMask is a self-custodial wallet interface. It lets you manage wallet keys and interact with blockchains; it is not a bank account.

Can MetaMask recover my Secret Recovery Phrase?

No. MetaMask cannot retrieve a lost SRP. If you still have access to your wallet, follow official instructions to back it up securely.

Is MetaMask free to use?

Downloading the wallet is free, but blockchain transactions generally require network fees. Swaps and other in-wallet services may also have service or provider fees.

Does connecting to a dApp give it my crypto?

A basic connection can expose your public address and let a dApp request actions. Moving tokens generally requires a transaction or a previously granted token allowance.

Which networks does MetaMask support?

The current network guide lists Ethereum, Bitcoin, Solana, Tron, Linea, Base, Polygon, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Monad, Robinhood Chain, OP, Sei, Avalanche and more. Features vary by network.

Can I use MetaMask on Android and iPhone?

Yes. MetaMask provides mobile apps. Use the official download page to reach the correct app-store listing and verify the publisher before installing.

What is the difference between disconnecting and revoking?

Disconnecting removes the dApp’s wallet connection. Revoking removes an on-chain token allowance. Disconnecting alone does not revoke previous approvals.

Does MetaMask support Ledger and Trezor?

MetaMask documents hardware-wallet integrations including Ledger and Trezor. Exact device compatibility differs between Extension and Mobile.

Are MetaMask transactions reversible?

Most confirmed blockchain transactions cannot be reversed by MetaMask. Verify the network, destination address, token and transaction details before signing.
