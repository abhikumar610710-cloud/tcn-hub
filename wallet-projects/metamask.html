🦊 1. What is MetaMask? (Overview)

MetaMask is a self-custody cryptocurrency wallet that helps people manage supported digital assets and connect to decentralized applications (DApps). It is available as a browser extension and mobile app, letting users interact with supported blockchain networks, review transactions, and manage their wallet accounts.

A self-custody wallet means you are responsible for protecting your Secret Recovery Phrase and approving transactions. MetaMask does not make every blockchain application safe, so always verify websites, network details, and transaction requests before proceeding.

✅ Advantages

Browser and mobile access, self-custody wallet model, seamless DApp connectivity, and support for multiple EVM networks.

⚠️ Things to Consider

You must protect recovery details yourself, phishing sites can imitate wallets, blockchain transactions may be irreversible, and network fees apply.

💡 Quick Fact: MetaMask is a wallet interface, not a bank account. Your assets are recorded on the relevant blockchain, while your wallet helps you manage access and sign transactions.

💸 3. Fee & Gas Structure

Creating and holding a MetaMask wallet is free, though blockchain network gas fees and optional service fees apply when transacting:

Wallet Creation Free — creating and managing accounts inside MetaMask has no subscription charge.
Network Gas Fees Every on-chain send, swap, or smart-contract approval requires native gas tokens (such as ETH, POL, or BNB) paid to blockchain validators.
Built-In Swaps & On-Ramps Optional in-app MetaMask Swaps, Bridge, or third-party fiat on-ramp providers include service/provider fees displayed before confirmation.

🌐 4. Network Support

MetaMask supports Ethereum and a wide range of EVM-compatible blockchains, plus custom RPC networks and Snaps integrations:

Ethereum Mainnet Polygon BNB Smart Chain Arbitrum One Optimism Base Avalanche C-Chain Linea Custom EVM Networks

🗝️ 5. Custody & Control

MetaMask operates on a non-custodial (self-custody) model where your Secret Recovery Phrase and private keys are encrypted locally on your device.

No MetaMask support agent can access your wallet, reset your password without your recovery phrase, or reverse an approved on-chain transaction.

🛡️ 7. Security Tips

Protect your wallet credentials and devices using these essential self-custody practices:

Protect Your Secret Recovery Phrase Never share it with anyone, including people claiming to be support, and never type it into websites or forms.
Check Website Addresses Use the official metamask.io domain and beware of sponsored search results and lookalike phishing domains.
Review Every Transaction Understand what token approvals or signatures you are authorizing before clicking confirm.
Secure Your Device Use a strong device screen lock, keep your OS/browser updated, and consider hardware wallet pairing for larger balances.

❓ 10. Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is MetaMask free to use?

Creating a wallet is generally free. Network gas fees and fees for optional services or transactions may apply.

2. Is MetaMask safe?

MetaMask provides wallet security features, but safety also depends on your device, recovery phrase handling, and the sites or transactions you approve.

3. Can I use MetaMask on mobile?

MetaMask offers an official mobile app. Download it only from links provided by the official MetaMask website or verified app stores.

4. What if I lose my Secret Recovery Phrase?

If you lose access to your wallet and do not have the required recovery information, access may not be recoverable. Back it up securely before you need it.

5. Does MetaMask store my crypto?

Your assets exist on the relevant blockchain. MetaMask provides an interface and keys that let you interact with them.

6. Can MetaMask support recover my phrase?

No legitimate support agent should ask for your Secret Recovery Phrase. Anyone asking for it is a major red flag.
