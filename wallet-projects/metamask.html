What is MetaMask? — Understand Self-Custody

MetaMask is a self-custodial crypto wallet interface. It allows you to manage accounts and interact with supported blockchain networks and decentralized applications. It is available as a browser extension and as a mobile app for supported devices.

Your crypto assets are recorded on their respective blockchains; MetaMask gives you an interface to view supported assets, prepare transactions and approve actions with your wallet keys. MetaMask is not a traditional bank account and does not reverse completed blockchain transactions.

Self-custody means you are responsible for the Secret Recovery Phrase (SRP) and private keys that control your accounts. Your SRP can restore the wallet and its derived accounts. An individual private key controls one account, while the SRP is the master backup for the wallet.

Start here before depositing funds

If you lose access to your wallet and do not have a valid recovery method, MetaMask Support cannot retrieve your SRP or restore control of your on-chain assets. Anyone who gets your SRP or private key can potentially take control of the associated accounts.

How to add a supported network

1

Open the network selector

In the extension, open the network dropdown from the Tokens view. In mobile, use the network dropdown at the top of the Tokens screen.

2

Check the supported network list

Open Manage Networks or the additional networks list and search for the chain by name. Prefer networks already listed by MetaMask.

3

Add and confirm

Select the network and review the details before adding it. The app may let you add popular networks directly.

Be careful with custom RPC settings

Do not copy RPC URLs or chain details from random comments, messages or unknown websites. A wrong or malicious network configuration can mislead you. Verify custom network details with the network’s official documentation.

A token symbol can exist on different networks. Before sending or receiving, confirm the exact network, token contract, destination address and supported route. Never assume two assets with the same name are interchangeable.

Complete Setup Guide — Browser Extension and Mobile

Desktop browser setup

1

Visit the official download page

Type metamask.com/download yourself or use the green Get MetaMask button above. Check the domain carefully.

2

Choose your browser

Select the extension for a supported browser from the official store page reached through MetaMask’s website. Avoid third-party download mirrors.

3

Create a new wallet or restore an existing one

Follow the extension setup instructions. If restoring, enter the SRP only inside the genuine MetaMask app or extension you opened yourself.

4

Back up your SRP offline

Write the words in the correct order and store the backup in a private, secure place. Do not save it in email, chat, screenshots or shared cloud files.

5

Set a strong password

Use a unique password for the device/app and secure the computer with an updated operating system, screen lock and reputable security software.

Mobile setup

1

Use the official app-store link

Start at metamask.com/download and follow its link to the official iOS App Store or Google Play listing.

2

Create or import your wallet

Follow the app instructions. Never enter your SRP into a website or a message sent by someone claiming to be support.

3

Enable device protection

Use a strong phone passcode and available biometric protection. Keep the app and phone operating system updated.

New user tip

Before moving a large amount, learn how to copy an address, check the network and review a transaction. Consider a small test transfer first, while accounting for the extra network fee.

Security Center — Protect Your Secret Recovery Phrase

MetaMask’s security guidance is direct: never share your Secret Recovery Phrase or private keys with anyone, including someone claiming to be from MetaMask Support. Anyone with these credentials can control the associated accounts and move assets.

✍️

Write it down carefully

Record all words, spelling and order correctly. Keep the backup somewhere private and physically secure.

🚫

Never share it

Never type your SRP into a support form, website, chat, email or remote-help session. Real support will not ask you for it.

☁️

Avoid online copies

Avoid screenshots, cloud notes, email drafts and files synced to shared devices. These can be exposed if an account or device is compromised.

🔍

Check every transaction

Read the network, recipient, token, amount and approval details. Reject actions you do not understand or did not initiate.

🧹

Review token approvals

A token allowance may let a smart contract spend tokens up to an approved amount. Review and revoke unneeded allowances separately.

🧊

Consider a hardware wallet

A compatible hardware wallet keeps keys on a separate device and requires physical approval for transactions. It does not protect against every mistake.

MetaMask will not contact you first to request wallet secrets

MetaMask says its support team will never ask for your SRP or private key, and does not have a telephone support number. Treat unsolicited support messages and “wallet validation” requests as suspicious.

Recovery: what you should understand

If you still have access to an unlocked MetaMask wallet, follow the official instructions to view and back up the SRP securely. If you are setting up a new device or fresh install, the SRP can restore the wallet. If the SRP is lost and no accessible copy or supported recovery route remains, MetaMask cannot retrieve the phrase for you.

Connect to dApps — Accounts, Permissions and Approvals

A decentralized application (dApp) may ask to connect to MetaMask. A connection can allow the dApp to see the selected public wallet address and request actions. Connecting is not the same as giving away your SRP, but signing a message, approving a token allowance or submitting a transaction can have important consequences.

1

Verify the dApp address

Use the genuine project website, not a sponsored look-alike or link from an unexpected message.

2

Review the connection request

Check which account and network the dApp wants to access. Connect only the account you intend to use.

3

Read signatures and transaction prompts

A signature may authorize an off-chain action; a transaction changes blockchain state. Never approve an unexplained request.

4

Manage permissions later

Open MetaMask’s Permissions area to review connected dApps and which accounts they can access. Remove connections you no longer need.

5

Review token allowances separately

Disconnecting a dApp does not revoke token approvals already granted on-chain. Use a trusted approval checker or MetaMask Portfolio where supported.

Important distinction

“Disconnect” removes a wallet connection. “Revoke approval” removes a token-spending allowance. These are different actions and may each require a network transaction and fee.

Gas Fees, Swaps, Bridges and Transaction Costs

Many blockchain actions require a network fee (gas) to be processed. This fee is paid to the network, not simply to MetaMask. It can change with network activity, transaction complexity and the chain being used. Bitcoin and Solana have network fees too, although their fee models differ from Ethereum gas.

⛽

Network fee / Gas

Paid to process a transaction. A complex smart-contract interaction may cost more than a simple transfer.

🔄

MetaMask swap fee

MetaMask’s published standard fee is currently 0.875% for covered swap and cross-chain transactions; eligible promotions may reduce it.

🌉

Bridge provider fees

Cross-chain routes can include provider costs and network fees on the source and destination chains.

📉

Slippage and price impact

Low liquidity or price movement can change the final output. Review the quote and minimum received before you confirm.

Hardware Wallets — An Extra Signing Layer

MetaMask documents integrations with several hardware wallets, including Ledger, Trezor, Lattice and supported air-gapped devices. Availability differs between the browser extension and MetaMask Mobile, so check the current compatibility guide before buying a device.

🔐

Keys stay with the device

When configured correctly, the hardware wallet signs transactions without exposing its private key to the browser.

👆

Physical confirmation

You need access to the device to approve transactions. Review the address and amount on its own screen.

🧩

dApp compatibility

Connecting a hardware wallet through MetaMask can help use sites that support MetaMask but not a direct device connection.

⚠️

Not a complete shield

A hardware wallet cannot prevent every scam. You can still approve a malicious transaction or disclose your recovery phrase.

Advantages, Limitations and Who It May Suit

✅

Advantages

Self-custody, browser and mobile access, dApp connectivity, support for multiple networks, token and NFT management, swap features and hardware-wallet integrations.

⚠️

Limitations and risks

You are responsible for the SRP and private keys; phishing and malicious dApps remain risks; gas fees vary; and blockchain transfers are generally irreversible.

Who may find MetaMask useful?

MetaMask may suit users who want to manage supported crypto assets and interact with Web3 applications. Beginners should first learn account backups, network selection, transaction fees, token approvals and address verification. Start with low-risk practice before moving significant funds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MetaMask a bank or a centralized exchange?

No. MetaMask is a self-custodial wallet interface. You control the wallet credentials and interact with blockchain networks; it is not a bank account.

Can MetaMask recover my Secret Recovery Phrase?

No. MetaMask cannot retrieve a lost SRP. If you still have access to an unlocked wallet, follow the official secure backup instructions. Never share the phrase with support or anyone else.

Is MetaMask free to use?

Downloading the wallet is free, but blockchain transactions generally require network fees. Swaps and other in-wallet services may also have service or provider fees.

Does connecting to a dApp give it my crypto?

A basic connection can expose the selected public address and let a dApp request actions. Moving assets generally requires a transaction or an existing token allowance. Read every request.

Which networks does MetaMask support?

Its current network guide includes Ethereum, Bitcoin, Solana, Tron, Linea, Base, Polygon, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Optimism, Sei, Avalanche and additional networks. Features vary by chain.

Can I use MetaMask on Android and iPhone?

Yes. MetaMask offers mobile apps. Use the official download page to reach the correct app-store listing and verify the publisher before installing.

What is the difference between disconnecting and revoking?

Disconnecting removes the dApp’s wallet connection. Revoking removes an on-chain token allowance. Disconnecting alone does not revoke approvals previously granted.

Does MetaMask support Ledger and Trezor?

MetaMask documents support for Ledger, Trezor and other hardware wallets. Exact device compatibility differs between Extension and Mobile.

Are MetaMask transactions reversible?

Most confirmed blockchain transactions cannot be reversed by MetaMask. Carefully verify the network, destination address, token and transaction details before signing.
