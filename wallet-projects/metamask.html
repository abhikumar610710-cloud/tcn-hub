1 What is MetaMask?

MetaMask is a self-custody cryptocurrency wallet that helps people manage supported digital assets and connect to decentralized applications (DApps). It is available as a browser extension and mobile app. With MetaMask, users can interact with supported blockchain networks, review transactions and manage their wallet accounts.

A self-custody wallet means you are responsible for protecting your Secret Recovery Phrase and approving transactions. MetaMask does not make every blockchain application safe, so always verify websites, network details and transaction requests before proceeding.

💡

Quick Fact

MetaMask is a wallet interface, not a bank account. Your assets are recorded on the relevant blockchain, while your wallet helps you manage access and sign transactions.

5 Security Tips

  • 🛡️Protect your Secret Recovery Phrase. Never share it with anyone, including people claiming to be support.
  • 🔎Check website addresses. Use the official domain and beware of sponsored search results and lookalikes.
  • ✋Review every transaction. Understand what you are signing before approving.
  • 🔐Secure your device. Use a screen lock, updates and trusted security practices.

7 Frequently Asked Questions

Creating a wallet is generally free. Network gas fees and fees for optional services or transactions may apply.
MetaMask provides wallet security features, but safety also depends on your device, recovery phrase handling and the sites or transactions you approve.
MetaMask offers an official mobile app. Download it only from links provided by the official MetaMask website or verified app stores.
If you lose access to your wallet and do not have the required recovery information, access may not be recoverable. Back it up securely before you need it.
Your assets exist on the relevant blockchain. MetaMask provides an interface and keys that let you interact with them.
No legitimate support agent should ask for your Secret Recovery Phrase. Anyone asking for it is a major red flag.
