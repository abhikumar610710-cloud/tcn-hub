🦊 1. Overview — What is MetaMask?

MetaMask is a widely used self-custody (non-custodial) cryptocurrency wallet designed for managing digital assets and connecting to decentralized applications (DApps) across Ethereum and EVM-compatible blockchains. Available both as a browser extension and a mobile app, it lets you hold tokens, view NFTs, swap assets, and sign Web3 transactions.

Because MetaMask is non-custodial, you generate and hold your own 12-word Secret Recovery Phrase. Neither MetaMask nor any third party stores your funds—your assets live directly on the blockchain, while MetaMask acts as your secure key manager and Web3 interface.

✅ Key Advantages

  • Full self-custody control over your private keys and wallet accounts.
  • Seamless one-click connection with thousands of DeFi, NFT, and Web3 DApps.
  • Available across both desktop browsers and iOS/Android mobile devices.
  • Supports multiple EVM networks, custom RPCs, and hardware wallet pairing.

⚠️ Things to Consider

  • You are 100% responsible for backing up your 12-word Secret Recovery Phrase offline.
  • Phishing websites and fake browser extensions often imitate MetaMask.
  • On-chain blockchain transfers are irreversible once confirmed.
  • Every on-chain transaction requires native gas fees (e.g., ETH, POL, BNB).
💡 Quick Fact: MetaMask is a wallet interface, not a bank account. Your coins and tokens are recorded on the public blockchain ledger, while your wallet stores the cryptographic keys needed to authorize transfers.

⭐ 2. Key Features

MetaMask combines everyday crypto storage with built-in Web3 interaction and portfolio management tools:

🛡️ Self-Custody Key Vault

Your private keys are encrypted locally on your device with your wallet password. You can create multiple accounts under one Secret Recovery Phrase or import existing private keys.

🌐 Multi-Network & Custom RPC

Switch effortlessly between Ethereum Mainnet, Layer-2 rollups, and sidechains—or add any custom EVM-compatible blockchain using its Network Name, RPC URL, and Chain ID.

🔁 Built-In Swaps & Bridge

Compare quotes across decentralized exchanges (DEXs) directly inside MetaMask to swap tokens or bridge assets across supported networks without manually hunting for liquidity pools.

🧩 DApp Connect & Snaps

Connect safely to DeFi protocols, Web3 games, and NFT marketplaces, review human-readable signature prompts, and extend wallet features via MetaMask Snaps.

📊 MetaMask Portfolio

Track token balances, multi-chain holdings, staking options, and transaction history across multiple accounts from a single dashboard view.

🔒 Hardware Wallet Pairing

Connect cold storage devices such as Ledger, Trezor, Keystone, or Lattice1 so private keys stay offline while using MetaMask's Web3 interface.

💸 3. Fee & Gas Structure

Downloading and creating a MetaMask wallet is completely free, while blockchain transactions and optional built-in services carry network or provider fees:

Wallet Download & Setup Free (0 Fee) — Creating unlimited wallet accounts and receiving crypto transfers incurs no MetaMask charge.
Blockchain Network Gas Fees Every on-chain send, token approval, or smart-contract call requires gas paid in the network's native coin (e.g., ETH on Ethereum, POL on Polygon, BNB on BNB Chain). You can adjust gas speed (Low, Market, Aggressive) before confirming.
MetaMask Swaps & Bridge Using the built-in Swap or Cross-Chain Bridge aggregator includes a transparent service fee (typically around 0.875% on Swaps) shown clearly in the quote preview alongside estimated gas.
Fiat Buy / Sell On-Ramps Buying crypto with cards, UPI/bank transfers, or local payment providers routes through third-party partners whose processing fees and exchange spreads are displayed prior to checkout.

🗝️ 5. Custody & Control Model

MetaMask follows a strict non-custodial (self-custody) architecture. Understanding how your keys, password, and recovery phrase work is essential for protecting your funds:

1️⃣ Secret Recovery Phrase

A 12-word master backup generated when you create your wallet. Anyone with these 12 words can restore and control all accounts derived from it on any device.

2️⃣ Local Wallet Password

Unlocks the MetaMask app or extension on your current device only. If you forget your password, you can reset it using your 12-word Secret Recovery Phrase.

3️⃣ Account Private Keys

Each individual wallet address (Account 1, Account 2, etc.) has its own unique cryptographic private key used to sign transactions on-chain.

🛠️ 6. How to Set Up & Use MetaMask (Step-by-Step)

Follow these step-by-step instructions to create your MetaMask wallet, back it up properly, and start interacting with Web3 safely:

1

Step 1: Open the Official MetaMask Website

Type https://metamask.io directly into your browser address bar. Avoid clicking sponsored search ads to protect yourself from clone websites.

2

Step 2: Install the Official Extension or Mobile App

Click Download and select your browser (Chrome, Brave, Firefox, Edge) or mobile store (Google Play / Apple App Store), then pin the extension for quick access.

3

Step 3: Create a New Wallet & Strong Password

Select Create a new wallet, review the terms, and set a strong, unique device password (at least 12+ characters) that you do not use anywhere else.

4

Step 4: Write Down Your 12-Word Secret Recovery Phrase Offline

Reveal your 12 words in exact numbered order, write them neatly on paper (or a metal backup plate), complete the confirmation quiz, and store the backup in a locked physical location—never in screenshots, email, or cloud notes.

5

Step 5: Receive Funds, Switch Networks & Connect Safely

Copy your public wallet address (0x...) to receive supported tokens, ensure you hold a small amount of the network's native gas token, and test with a small amount before making larger transfers or connecting to DApps.

🛡️ 7. Security Best Practices

Because self-custody gives you complete ownership, following strong security habits prevents the most common Web3 losses:

Never Share Your 12 Words No legitimate MetaMask team member, moderator, or DApp will ever ask for your Secret Recovery Phrase or private key—never enter it into any website form or support chat.
Verify Every URL & Bookmark Bookmark official DApps and metamask.io. Double-check domain spelling before clicking Connect Wallet.
Inspect Signatures & Approvals Read transaction simulation details carefully. Avoid signing blind messages or granting unlimited token spending approvals to unverified smart contracts.
Use Burner & Vault Wallets Keep long-term savings in a separate hardware-backed vault wallet, and use a dedicated daily wallet with limited funds for minting, airdrops, or testing new DApps.
Keep Device & Browser Clean Install only trusted browser extensions, keep your OS and MetaMask version updated, and lock your wallet when stepping away from your PC or phone.

❓ 10. Frequently Asked Questions (Detailed Guide)

1. Is MetaMask free to download and use?

Yes: Downloading the MetaMask extension or mobile app, creating wallets, and receiving crypto are completely free.

When fees apply: Sending crypto, interacting with smart contracts, or swapping tokens requires blockchain network gas fees paid to validators, plus an optional service fee if you use MetaMask's built-in Swap or Bridge aggregator.

2. What is the difference between my Public Address, Private Key, and Secret Recovery Phrase?

Public Address (0x...): Safe to share with others so they can send you tokens—similar to an email address or UPI ID.

Private Key: Controls a single specific account inside your wallet; never share it with anyone.

Secret Recovery Phrase (12 words): The master key that generates and restores all accounts inside your wallet. Anyone who gets your 12 words gains full control of all funds across every derived account.

3. Does MetaMask store my crypto on its servers?

No: MetaMask is a non-custodial wallet interface. Your tokens and NFTs always remain recorded on the public blockchain (such as Ethereum or Polygon).

What MetaMask stores: Your encrypted cryptographic keys locally on your device so you can view balances and sign transactions.

4. Can I use the same MetaMask wallet on both PC and mobile?

Yes: During setup on your second device, select Import an existing wallet and carefully enter your 12-word Secret Recovery Phrase.

Important note: Always download the mobile app or browser extension only from official links on metamask.io/download, and remember that custom networks or imported tokens may need to be re-enabled on the new device.

5. Why are my tokens not showing up after a transfer?

Check the active network: Make sure MetaMask is switched to the exact blockchain network used for the transfer (for example, Polygon, Arbitrum, or BNB Chain instead of Ethereum Mainnet).

Import custom token: Some ERC-20 tokens do not appear automatically until you click Import tokens and paste the official token contract address verified on a block explorer like Etherscan.

6. What happens if I lose my Secret Recovery Phrase or forget my password?

Forgot password (still have 12 words): You can reinstall or reset MetaMask and restore full access using your 12-word Secret Recovery Phrase.

Lost 12 words (and locked out): Because MetaMask does not store your keys, nobody—including MetaMask Support—can recover or reset a wallet without the Secret Recovery Phrase.

7. How do I revoke old DApp permissions and token approvals?

Why it matters: When you interact with DeFi protocols or DEXs, you often grant smart contracts permission to spend a specific token.

How to manage: You can review connected sites inside MetaMask's Connected sites menu and use reputable token-approval checkers (such as Etherscan's Token Approval Checker or Revoke.cash) to revoke unused allowances.

8. Can MetaMask support ever DM me or ask for my recovery phrase?

Never: Legitimate MetaMask support agents will never DM you first on Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, or X, and will never ask for your 12-word phrase, private key, or remote screen access. Treat any such message as a scam immediately.
