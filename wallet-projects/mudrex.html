1 What is Mudrex?

Mudrex is a crypto trading and investing platform that offers spot trading, futures, curated Coin Sets, crypto SIPs and access to Web3 tokens. Its official website currently describes access to 650+ crypto assets and reports that over 30 lakh Indians use its platform. These are company-published figures and may change.

For Indian users, the platform describes INR funding and INR-denominated trading alongside USDT markets. Product availability, limits and eligibility can vary by account and region, so confirm the current details inside the official app before placing an order.

💡

Quick Fact

Mudrex is a custodial trading platform, not a self-custody wallet like MetaMask or Trust Wallet. Use the official app/site, secure your account and understand withdrawal and custody terms.

2 Key Products & Features

📈

Spot Trading

Buy and sell supported crypto assets through INR-native spot markets. Mudrex's site currently advertises 650+ crypto assets.

⚖️

Web3 Tokens

Commodity Perpetuals

5 Security & Compliance

  • 🧾FIU-India registration. Mudrex's compliance page lists RPFAS Technologies Private Limited with FIU registration number VA00031079. FIU registration is not a guarantee against investment loss.
  • 🔐Account protection. Mudrex describes 2FA, email confirmations, AES-256 encryption and restricted access controls.
  • 🏅Security standards. Mudrex's website displays ISO 27001:2022 and SOC 2 Type II claims; consult its compliance page for current details.
  • 🚫Never share credentials. No legitimate support agent should ask for your password, PIN, OTP, API secret or recovery phrase.
  • 🔎Verify official channels. Check the official website and app-store listings; avoid APKs or payment requests sent through chat.
