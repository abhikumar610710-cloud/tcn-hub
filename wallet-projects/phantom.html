1 What is Phantom Wallet?

Phantom is a self-custody crypto wallet that helps people manage supported digital assets and connect to decentralized applications (DApps). It began with a strong Solana focus and has expanded to support several additional networks, including Ethereum, Bitcoin and selected EVM chains.

Phantom is available as a mobile app and browser extension. You can create a wallet using a Secret Recovery Phrase or, where offered, sign in with a Google or Apple account. In either case, protect your recovery credentials: Phantom cannot access your funds or restore a wallet if you lose the required credentials.

💡

Quick Fact

Phantom can show assets across enabled networks in one wallet experience. Network balances remain separate, and you need the correct network's native token to pay its transaction fees.

⚠️ Check the network before transferring

Phantom's current help guide lists Arbitrum, Optimism, Avalanche, Unichain and Linea as unsupported. Do not send tokens on an unsupported network to a Phantom address. Also remember that assets and gas balances are separate on each network.

6 Security Tips

  • 🛡️Never share your Secret Recovery Phrase. Phantom will not ask you to send it to support or enter it on a website.
  • 🔎Verify the download source. Use phantom.com/download and check the publisher before installing an extension.
  • ✋Review every signature. Check transaction details, token approvals and connected websites before approving.
  • ⚠️Ignore unsolicited support messages. Phantom says support will not contact you first or ask for sensitive wallet information.
  • 🌐Confirm the network. A similar-looking address does not mean two chains are interchangeable.
