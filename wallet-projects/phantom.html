👻 1. Overview

Phantom is a self-custody (non-custodial) crypto wallet that helps you manage supported digital assets and connect to decentralized applications (dApps). Originally known for a strong Solana focus, Phantom has expanded to support several additional networks including Ethereum, Bitcoin, and selected EVM chains.

Phantom is available on mobile and desktop browsers. You can create a wallet with a Secret Recovery Phrase or, where supported, sign in with a Google or Apple account—while still keeping full responsibility for protecting your recovery credentials because Phantom cannot access your funds or restore a lost wallet without the required credentials.

What It Helps You Do

  • Multi-Chain Asset & NFT Management: View and manage supported tokens and NFTs across enabled networks, interact with dApps, and access built-in swap or staking features where available.
  • Flexible Onboarding: Create a wallet using a Secret Recovery Phrase or supported Google/Apple sign-in flows.

What to Keep in Mind

  • Separate Chain Balances & Gas: Network balances stay separate on each chain, and each network needs its own native gas token for transactions.
  • Unsupported Chain Transfers: Sending funds on an unsupported network risks permanent loss.

⭐ 2. Key Features

Phantom brings multi-chain asset viewing, dApp connectivity, and transaction checks into one wallet interface:

  • Multi-Chain & Cross-Network View: Supports Solana, Ethereum, Bitcoin, and selected EVM networks, showing assets across enabled chains without manually switching wallets each time.
  • Transaction Insights & Security Alerts: Provides transaction details and available security warnings before you sign requests or connect to applications.
  • NFTs, Swaps & Staking: Lets you view supported digital collectibles, swap supported assets, and access staking options depending on network and product availability.
  • Hardware Wallet Support: Supports connecting a compatible Ledger hardware device to manage accounts and sign supported transactions.

💸 3. Fee

Knowing how wallet costs work across chains and actions helps you plan transactions smoothly:

Wallet Download & Creation Downloading Phantom and creating or importing a wallet is free; there is no recurring subscription fee just to hold a wallet.
Network / Gas Fees Sending tokens, interacting with smart contracts, or submitting transactions requires network fees paid in the active network's native asset (such as SOL on Solana or ETH on Ethereum).
Swap, Trade & Bridge Fees Using swap, trading, or cross-chain features may include network gas, provider/swap fees, or slippage/spread—always review the exact quote before confirming.
Network-Specific Gas Balances Each chain has its own separate balance and fee token; having gas on one network does not pay gas on another.

🗝️ 5. Custody & Control

Phantom is a self-custody wallet, giving you direct control over your wallet credentials rather than relying on a custodial exchange:

  • Secret Recovery Phrase & Sign-In Recovery: Whether you set up with a Secret Recovery Phrase or a supported Google/Apple sign-in flow, safeguarding your recovery credentials is essential.
  • No Custodial Recovery by Support: Phantom cannot access your funds or restore a wallet if you lose the credentials required to recover it, and confirmed transactions cannot simply be reversed.

🛡️ 7. Security

Strong self-custody security combines safe wallet habits with careful verification on every transaction:

Use a Secondary Wallet Use a secondary wallet with limited funds when trying new dApps, mints, or unfamiliar links so your main holdings stay isolated.
Never Share Recovery Phrase Never share your Secret Recovery Phrase or recovery credentials with anyone—Phantom will not ask you to send them to support or enter them on a website.
Ignore Unsolicited Support Be wary of unsolicited messages claiming to be support; Phantom notes that support will not reach out first or ask for sensitive wallet info.
Review Signatures & Networks Check transaction details, token approvals, connected sites, and network selection before signing—a similar-looking address format does not make chains interchangeable.

❓ 10. FAQ

1. Is Phantom Wallet free to download?

What it is: Yes—downloading Phantom from its official website is free, while blockchain transactions, swaps, and third-party services can involve network or protocol fees.

How to check: Always review the network gas and swap/transaction details shown before confirming an action.

2. Does Phantom support only Solana?

What it is: No—Phantom supports Solana along with Ethereum, Bitcoin, and selected EVM networks, though supported networks can change over time.

How to check: Confirm current network support in Phantom's official guide and wallet settings before transferring assets.

3. Which networks are unsupported on Phantom?

What to know: Phantom's help guide lists Arbitrum, Optimism, Avalanche, Unichain, and Linea as unsupported.

How to stay safe: Never send funds on unsupported networks to a Phantom address, as transfers on unsupported networks risk loss.

4. Can I use Phantom on both mobile and desktop?

What it is: Yes—Phantom provides iOS and Android apps as well as a Chrome extension (and may run in other Chromium browsers).

How to set up: Download only from phantom.com/download and verify the official extension/app listing.

5. Can I create a wallet without a Secret Recovery Phrase?

What it is: Phantom offers Google or Apple sign-in onboarding in supported versions, while a Secret Recovery Phrase remains an important backup method.

How to stay safe: Follow Phantom's current backup instructions and keep recovery details private and secure.

6. Why should I use a secondary wallet and verify signatures?

What it is: A secondary wallet separates experimental dApp activity from your main funds, and signature prompts authorize contract actions or token permissions.

How to stay safe: Use a secondary wallet on unfamiliar dApps, inspect every transaction/approval request, and never share your recovery phrase.

7. Can Phantom support recover my funds or Secret Recovery Phrase?

What to know: Because Phantom is self-custodial, it cannot access your funds or restore a wallet if you lose the required recovery credentials.

How to stay safe: Never share your Secret Recovery Phrase with anyone claiming to be support and double-check every transfer before sending.
