Ecosystem Architecture What are Web3 Campaigns and How Does Airdrop Allocation Work?

Web3 Campaigns & Protocol Airdrops enable decentralized networks to bootstrap early liquidity and community governance with Zero Capital Investment. Instead of spending capital, participants earn allocation shares by stress-testing testnet smart contracts, claiming daily ecosystem points, or completing verified quests across reputable platforms such as Galxe, TaskOn, and Zealy.

What Do Users Need to Do?
Users participate by requesting free testnet faucets to test swaps, bridging, or daily social quests. There is no financial purchase required; simply interact using a dedicated non-custodial Web3 wallet (MetaMask or Phantom) and build active on-chain history.
How Do Web3 Campaigns Work?
Emerging L1/L2 blockchains and dApps allocate dedicated marketing pools. By partnering with quest hubs like Galxe and TaskOn, they track user credentials through on-chain badges (OATs), Discord roles, and XP point leaderboards ahead of mainnet deployment.
Reward Distribution & Claiming
Upon reaching the official Token Generation Event (TGE), verified campaign points convert into tradeable crypto tokens based on snapshot criteria. Tokens are claimed directly to user wallets and can be converted into liquid assets on major exchanges.
Total Crypto Network Audit Policy
Phishing protection is strictly enforced. The research desk at Total Crypto Network audits every contract link to prevent wallet drainer scripts, verifying that listed campaigns never request private keys or high-risk fund approvals.
