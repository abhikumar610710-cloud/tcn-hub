Token Checker & Contract Audit

Inspect smart contracts, verify official token addresses, and avoid honeypots

Anti-Scam Architecture Why Contract Verification is Mandatory in Web3 & Airdrops

In decentralized trading, malicious actors frequently launch counterfeit clone tokens and honeypots mimicking legitimate Web3 projects. Interacting with unverified contracts can lead to permanent loss of capital through hidden sales taxes or frozen selling functions. Total Crypto Hub provides this neutral verification suite to help users inspect contracts before connecting wallets or approving liquidity swaps.

🍯 Honeypot & Sell Restrictions
Malicious contracts permit buying but block token sales by reverting sell transactions. Always check contract functions to ensure open liquidity withdrawal.
🎭 Counterfeit Token Clones
Scammers issue fake tokens with identical tickers on decentralized exchanges. Always confirm the official contract address (CA) from verified protocol documentation.
📉 Hidden Buy / Sell Taxes
Predatory developers embed 50% to 99% transfer fees directly inside token contracts, draining your swap value without prior warning on decentralized front-ends.
🔒 Liquidity Lock & Mint Control
Verify whether the protocol's liquidity pool (LP) is permanently burned or time-locked, and whether the developer retains the authority to arbitrarily mint new tokens.
